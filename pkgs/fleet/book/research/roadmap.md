## Integrating new work

A discrepancy belongs on the owning issue with a source, a bounded change, its oracle and the person or system that can supply missing evidence. Check the governing hold and the latest claim before implementation and again before merge. Ship via an isolated worktree and immutable verification ref. Preserve the previous generation and closure when a deploy is part of the work.

Do not infer throughput from nominal slot counts. The three provider surfaces can share bottlenecks. Record first eligible time, dispatch acceptance, runner assignment, job start, completion and observation. A waiting submitter has no GitHub workflow time yet. A job that fails before its test process starts supplies no assertion verdict.

Historical investigations and live dispositions are dated. Native dependency edges, body-level acceptance requirements, queue projections and human holds can change independently. An issue's priority is actionable only after those four records agree or their difference has an owner.
