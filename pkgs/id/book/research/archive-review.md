## Two areas the original search missed

The March media report and peer-discovery records refer to their feature names and old source layout. Their headings and sibling files identify the media and federation work. Use their implementation tables as historical evidence and reconcile the paths before documenting current support. The November graph-native currency record points directly to #5522 and the governing #2043 design. Its scope is one implementation slice.

The October 11 events and delegated-authority design and the remaining-work ledger each contain extensive research artifacts. Group their supporting files with the parent record. Track the oracle, version and authority of a result before using it as a release claim. Individual scratch, database or key-format artifacts need no editorial change merely because their parent is relevant.

## Scope of the screen

The table in the companion CSV has exactly one row per archive path. Headings were extracted from the beginning of Markdown, reStructuredText and text files where available (982 headings). File names and ancestry supplied the evidence for data, source and binary artifacts. Cross-program references remain candidates for section-level review. A proposed topic mapping or a title containing “decision” alone establishes no governing authority.
