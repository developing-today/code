# Discovering a world, contacting its members and contesting a result

*Research snapshot of November 23, 2026. Code and issue observations refer to the cited revisions and collection times.*

A public listing may tell a traveler how to reach a world. It grants no right to enter, deliver an unsolicited message, receive group history or publish an opponent’s match evidence. Those decisions belong to different people and can have different answers. This chapter follows a traveler from finding a world through a disputed tournament result. Proposed acceptance examples are design work. Code references point to the inspected main commit `7310a957`.

## Finding a world without publishing its participants

ID already distinguishes temporary sightings of nearby peers, address lookup for a known endpoint, a signed world announcement and a registry curator’s signed inclusion decision. Iroh’s own address-lookup explanation makes the distinction concrete: a caller starts with a known endpoint ID and wants a dialable address. The older BitTorrent DHT design likewise stores contact information. Neither result establishes a world’s identity, its current membership or the right to join. [ID peer sightings](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-core/src/discovery.rs) · [ID DHT policy](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-sync/src/dht.rs) · [Iroh address lookup](https://www.iroh.computer/blog/iroh-0-96-0-the-quic-multipaths-to-1-0) · [BEP 5](https://www.bittorrent.org/beps/bep_0005.html)

The v1 design fixes resolution order: exact invite, pkarr for a known world key, then subscribed registries for browsing. Apply signature, increasing sequence and expiry at every applicable layer. A private world has no announcement. An unlisted world can be reached by key and does not go into a registry. A listed world chooses its registries. A fresh self-host subscribes to none. Direct connection can expose the joiner’s IP to the peer, and the joiner owns the relay-required setting. An announcement contains a relay hint. The underlying endpoint DHT setting has a separate scope: one world opting in enables lookup/publication for the shared endpoint, opt-out takes effect on rebind and a published record can outlive opt-out until its TTL. The operator needs both scopes displayed before changing a shared endpoint setting. [Discovery decision §§3–7](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/doc/2026-11-16_design_id6287_discovery_registry_federation_v1.md) · [World announcements](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-sync/src/announcement.rs) · [Endpoint DHT](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-sync/src/dht.rs)

**Proposed resolver fixture.** Give an operator three worlds, an exact private invite, a valid unlisted announcement and a stale registry entry for a listed world. Use a fake clock and call-recording sources. Check which data is disclosed to each source as well as what comes back. A private invite must not cause its world identifier to be submitted to browse/search. A later-sequence, expired record is unusable. A lower sequence cannot replace an accepted higher sequence. An authenticated equal-sequence, different-payload pair should preserve the cached record and record local conflict evidence without arbitrarily promoting a candidate. That last case is a proposed diagnostic; the existing decision supplies the monotone replacement rule. With relay-required enabled and the relay unreachable the scripted connector must record zero direct attempts. Put public discoverability, actual dialability and access to the world in separate result fields. [Resolver #10708](https://github.com/developing-today/code/issues/10708) · [Client configuration #10709](https://github.com/developing-today/code/issues/10709)

**Curator accountability.** A registry signs which announcements it includes and preserves the world’s own signed bytes. Mirroring applies the importing curator’s policy and tombstones. A useful v2 search result therefore gives the world key, unexpired announcement sequence, curator key and that curator’s current action. Two worlds claiming the same name remain distinct. A delisted world can still exist and another curator can retain it. For union search show each inclusion basis. For intersection require all named curators to include the world as of the displayed snapshots. A tombstone without an accessible reason remains a delisting; avoid copying personal allegations into a public index by default. Use a signed reason category with a restricted evidence reference where the curator’s policy allows. This is a presentation proposal for #10712, which already owns signal, decay and cross-registry search design. [Registry code](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-sync/src/registry.rs) · [Registry endpoint #10712](https://github.com/developing-today/code/issues/10712)

## Consent and confidentiality across routes

| Operation | Principal who decides | What the caller may conclude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resolve an address or browse a listing | World publisher and each selected curator control their statements. | A particular source supplied an address or signed listing at a given time. |
| Ask for contact | Sender signs a request. Recipient’s intake policy gates its admission. | Admission can establish a pending request. It supplies no evidence that the recipient read it. |
| Accept a relationship | Recipient explicitly accepts under the existing signed friend-envelope rules. | The relationship has an acceptance record. Determine each new content grant under its own policy. |
| Deliver encrypted group content | Authorized sender addresses the group under the negotiated scheme and group event state. | A member with the appropriate session and history grant may read it. Relay and recipient observations have separate meanings. |
| Export evidence to a tournament or curator | Relevant content grant, evidence policy and chosen registry govern the disclosure. | The receiver may evaluate what the evidence supports under its published policy. |

The directory signs friend requests and acceptances and defines a 30-day intake TTL, five-minute future-skew allowance, and up to 20 pending requests per sender per server. These constants apply to that directory path. The group encryption adapter has a session per device and group, consumes the folded membership model, supports full or bounded recent history, and rotates on explicit group operations. It includes a notice for a group created without a scheme. A signed envelope, an encrypted group message and a routed queue entry are three different objects. An author signature alone gives no content confidentiality. This source survey does not establish that all private inbox UI routes compose those pieces into an end-to-end encrypted service. [Friend envelopes](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-sync/src/envelope.rs) · [Directory admission](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-directory/src/directory.rs) · [Group encryption](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-groups/src/encryption/mod.rs)

Signal’s first-contact flow puts accept, delete and block next to an unfamiliar sender. Matrix Federation separates persistent events, ephemeral pairwise updates and point-in-time queries. MLS assigns responsibility for conflicting epoch changes and bounded delayed-message handling to a specific application integration. ID can use those distinctions while retaining its existing group scheme and decided protocol classes. A friend request should expose enough signed information for a recipient to decide, with no automatic attachment fetch, disclosure of read status or group-history grant. A relay-required path needs an explicit statement of who can observe addressing, timing and volume. End-to-end encryption does not hide all three. [Signal message requests](https://signal.org/blog/message-requests/) · [Matrix server-server API](https://spec.matrix.org/v1.18/server-server-api/) · [RFC 9420 §§14–15](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9420.html)

**Proposed channel checks.** Use the same two-device conversation through direct and authorized intermediary routes. Verify independently whether plaintext is visible in each role’s stored representation, whether the recipient’s accepted grant applies, and whether a deleted request stays deleted after old evidence is replayed. Test bounded history for a newly admitted member and fresh post-removal content for a removed member under the fixture’s scheme. An old device may retain material it already received. In a “scheme: none” group show the plaintext warning before send. If private messaging requires an encrypted policy, refuse sending through a route that cannot satisfy it. A recipient-local block should suppress future presentation, inbound resource use should remain bounded, and remote error classes should avoid exposing private account or block status. These checks require a declared policy for the inbox being exercised. A test of group encryption alone cannot establish the behavior of a separate inbox.

## A relay, a mailbox and an auditor promise different things

The chained-message protocol binds the origin signature and immutable envelope to its message ID. Hop records are independently signed and attached outside the envelope. The relay gate evaluates local membership before forwarding. Live frames have a distinct, bounded session contract. Matrix’s durable versus ephemeral distinction illustrates why a typing indication, consent transition and match objection deserve different retention classes. The Bundle Protocol’s status reports distinguish reception, forwarding and delivery. Its core v7 changes moved custody transfer out of the core protocol. ID should use the promised operation as the label for a receipt. A relay hop cannot attest that a human read a message or that a storage provider committed to a retention term. [Signed envelope and hops](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-sync/src/chained_message.rs) · [Relay gate](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-sync/src/relay_gate.rs) · [Messaging design](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/doc/2026-11-01_design_id2036.md) · [RFC 9171 §§6, Appendix A](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9171.html)

A proposed private inbox profile would bind any mailbox authority to a recipient, purpose, expiry, size/count limits and accepted content scheme. Separate deposits from listing or reading the inbox. On first contact, store a bounded request and make an acceptance decision locally. On expiry, clear eligible pending material under the declared retention rule. An authorized audit projection may retain separately scoped signed evidence. Preserve the existing durable relay and retention routes while establishing whether the user-facing mailbox has this contract. No new transport or cryptographic suite follows from selecting those UX semantics.

## What a tournament can count

ID’s reviewed design binds game and rules, players, any designated witness and evidence requirements in the agreement. Immutable signed inputs are available for replay under their declared logic. Witness judgments and objections are separate signed objects. The v1 projector has `Proposed`, `Agreed`, `Final`, `Witnessed`, `Disputed` and `Void` states. Registry `CountingPolicy` separately checks accepted states, witness identities and logic hashes. The versioned CLI audit log separates integrity, replay and observed-metadata screening. The implementation includes a named reference tic-tac-toe adapter; support for another game requires an appropriate replay adapter and a declared evidence profile. [Attestation decision](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/doc/2026-11-01_design_id2039.md) · [V1 match/projector types](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-events/src/attestation/v1.rs) · [CLI log and audit report](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/crates/id-cli/src/match_audit.rs)

For design review, hand two independent registries the same signed transcript, a witness decision and a timely objection. One registry counts the eligible state under an explicit rule, another requires a qualifying full replay. Display the independent audit dimensions, reference agreement and whether the registry counts the match. Retain the objection in either result. With only self-reported clocks or round-trip times, the design does not allow a timing violation to be established. With only a local cutoff, there is no independently enforceable objection deadline. Restrict sensitive observations to the auditor profile specified by the agreement. Certificate Transparency offers a narrow historical analogy: inclusion and consistency proofs establish specific claims about a log. ID should similarly name exactly which evidence supports inclusion, replay, witness judgment or finality. This does not require a public log of private match transcripts. [Audit and privacy decision §§4–7](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/doc/2026-11-01_design_id2039.md) · [Certificate Transparency §2.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9162.html)

## Jurisdiction and community governance

The world publisher owns its signed announcement, the curator its listing, the recipient its consent, and the tournament its counting policy. A hosting operator has a separate placement commitment. A useful policy record identifies issuer, subject or resource, permitted action, constraint, effective interval and superseding decision. ODRL provides comparative vocabulary for permissions, constraints and duties. ID can present the local rule and evidence issuer directly without adopting another policy language. An operator’s configured jurisdiction, a running provider resource with verified restricted bindings, and a legal residency guarantee are different claims. An application should report only the claim its evidence supports. [ODRL Information Model](https://www.w3.org/TR/odrl-model/) · [ID jurisdiction decision](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/doc/2026-11-01_jurisdiction_aware_user_store_design.md) · [Worker provisioning](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/hosted-worker/src/provisioning.ts)

Proposed governance fixture: a curator delists a listed world, a provider begins a user-store migration, and a user presents an otherwise valid exact invite. Evaluate three outputs independently. The registry projects a signed delisting, exact resolution evaluates the world’s signed record and validity, and storage admission enforces the account’s authoritative jurisdiction and migration fence. Neither a curator tombstone nor an invite can relax the fence. A post-migration verified restore must preserve byte identities under the existing export contract and report the route’s epoch. An absent region binding refuses service before any new unrestricted object is derived. Do not infer physical placement from a successful local fixture. [Account directory](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/hosted-worker/src/account-directory.ts) · [Restricted routing](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/pkgs/id/hosted-worker/src/routing.ts) · [Store migration design](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/7310a95730791e66da98f7b0e13865930058a516/doc/2026-11-01_jurisdiction_aware_user_store_design.md)
