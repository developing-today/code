# The data crates

Source: primary sources are the `lib.rs` and `Cargo.toml` files of [pkgs/id/crates/id-core/](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/main/pkgs/id/crates/id-core/), [pkgs/id/crates/id-store/](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/main/pkgs/id/crates/id-store/), [pkgs/id/crates/id-events/](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/main/pkgs/id/crates/id-events/), [pkgs/id/crates/id-sync/](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/main/pkgs/id/crates/id-sync/), [pkgs/id/crates/id-directory/](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/main/pkgs/id/crates/id-directory/), and [pkgs/id/crates/id-groups/](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/main/pkgs/id/crates/id-groups/). This chapter is complete without following those links; they record provenance only.

Six crates hold the data layer under the world runtime: `id-core` (foundation leaves), `id-store` (blobs, chains, documents, tags, search), `id-events` (the event store and the group projection), `id-sync` (log sync, the meta protocol, envelopes, artifacts), `id-directory` (the home-server directory), and `id-groups` (group policy and enforcement). All six are reachable through the facade at `id::` paths (see [The id facade crate](facade.md) and the map in [The crates](crates.md)). The on-disk formats these crates own are inventoried in [Data stores and schemas](../reference/data-stores.md).

## id-core — 58 public modules

Purpose (from its `Cargo.toml`): "Foundation leaf modules of the id crate (Phase 1 of the pkgs/id crate split, #3447)". Its `lib.rs` states the layering rule directly: the modules here are the crate's leaves — everything in the binary crate depends on them, and they depended on almost nothing in it. At 79 `.rs` files it is the largest leaf crate by file count after `id-worlds` and `id-cli`.

Public modules: `abuse`, `access`, `admission_gate`, `append_list`, `append_list_client`, `array_address`, `array_order`, `array_shorthand`, `block_list`, `boundary`, `boundary_surfaces`, `canonical_cbor`, `connectivity`, `content_id`, `discovery`, `durable_write`, `frames`, `graph` (with submodules `append_list`, `attribute`, `cross_signing`, `cursor`, `delegation`, `deletion`, `query`, `record`, `space_binding`, `term`, `version`), `hash_profile`, `hex`, `holders`, `id_namespace`, `id_shape`, `id_uri`, `id_uri_format`, `id_uri_parse`, `intranet`, `ip_evidence`, `iroh_hash`, `json_unique`, `key_envelope`, `location_ref`, `membership` (with submodules `body`, `frontier`, `gate`, `layer`), `namespace_definition`, `numeric_order`, `numeric_value`, `port`, `rejection`, `residency`, `residency_inventory`, `sanctions`, `sanctions_store`, `seed_phrase`, `signed_envelope`, `swarm`, `sync_method`, `text`, `time`, `transport_advertisement`, `triples`, `tuple`, `typed_value`, and `waiting`. Feature-gated modules: `petname`, `screen_codec`, and `world_presentation` under `world`; `qr` under `web`; `test_forgeries` under any of test or the `test-utils` feature. Key types, functions, and constants: `KEY_FILE = ".iroh-key"`, `CLIENT_KEY_FILE = ".iroh-key-client"`, `SERVE_LOCK = ".iroh-serve.lock"`; `is_node_id` (a 64-hex check); `discovery` (`PeerDiscovery`, `PeerInfo`, `PeerAnnouncement`, `Defaults`, `resolve_config`, `defaults_provenance`, `MAX_PEERS` — the facade re-exports 17 items from this module alone); `port` (the 9110 port contract vocabulary, re-exported onward by `id-store`, `id-events`, and `id-worlds`); `tuple` (sort-preserving encoding); `canonical_cbor`; `frames` (`MAX_FRAME_BYTES`); `membership`; `holders`; `waiting`; `signed_envelope`; `key_envelope`; `seed_phrase`; and the `graph` record model.

Features: `default = ["world", "sandbox"]`; `world` adds `rmp-serde` and gates `petname`, `screen_codec`, and `world_presentation`; `sandbox = []`; `web` adds `qrcode` and gates `qr`; `test-utils = []`; `test-provider = []` (test-only, for the #1061 exit proof). Embedding example: the facade binary calls this crate by its direct path at `pkgs/id/src/main.rs:195` — `let provenance = id_core::discovery::defaults_provenance();` — and the integration suite reaches the same modules through facade paths, for example `id::waiting::WaitingLimits` in `pkgs/id/tests/first_app_poc.rs`.

## id-store — 26 public modules

Purpose (from its `Cargo.toml`): "Store layer of the id crate: store traits, blob store, event headers, documents, tags and search (pkgs/id crate split step 2a, #4405)". Its `lib.rs` records two vocabularies it owns for layering reasons: the event store's own types (`event_types`, `range`) sit below the event-store implementation in `id-events`, and the op vocabularies the signed header embeds (`chat`, `notes`, `note_links`, `session_checkpoint`, `issuer_rotation`, `policy`) live with `chain::ChainExt`, which carries them as fields.

Public modules: `admission`, `chain`, `chat`, `chat_anchor`, `content_store`, `documents`, `event_types`, `graph_index`, `identity`, `issuer_rotation`, `media`, `namespace_registry`, `note_blocks`, `note_checkpoints`, `note_links`, `notes`, `numeric_registry`, `policy`, `range`, `search`, `session_checkpoint`, `store`, `store_traits`, `tag_claims`, `tag_index`, and `tags`; plus `pub use id_core::{port, content_id}` and `pub use store::STORE_PATH`.

Key types and functions: `store::StoreType` (`Persistent(FsStore)` or `Ephemeral(MemStore)`), `open_store`, and `load_or_create_keypair`, with `STORE_PATH = ".iroh-store"`; `chain::{ChainExt, Event, sign}` — the signed event-header chain; `store_traits`, including `EventStoreBackend`, the consumer seam consumed via `SharedEventStore`; the admission hooks `PrepareHook`, `OutboxHook`, `Reauthorizer`, `OperationLedger`, `MemoryOperationLedger`, `PrepareDecision`, and `OpState`; the tag model in `tags`, `tag_index`, and `tag_claims`; `documents::{DocumentRef, DOCUMENT_KIND}`; and `search`, `media`, and the `notes` family. The `media` module's `MediaStream` assembles streamed content under a credit window and enforces the 256 MiB artifact ceiling (`MAX_MEDIA_BYTES`) cumulatively during assembly: a frame that would take the assembled bytes over the ceiling is refused with the artifact oversize refusal before it consumes credit or extends the buffer, so the total is not only checked when the stream finishes into an artifact.

Features: `default = []` only. Dependents: the facade, `id-cli`, `id-events`, `id-groups`, `id-sync`, `id-web`, and `id-worlds`. This crate also dev-depends on the facade: its `store_traits` tests run against the real `EventStore`, and a comment in `crates/id-store/Cargo.toml` states that production code in `id-store` never names the facade.

Embedding example: the facade `lib.rs` quick-start is this crate's surface reached through `id::` paths — `open_store(false).await?`, `store.as_store().blobs().add_bytes(…)`, `store.as_store().tags().set(…)`. The tag commands in [Tutorial: organise a library with tags and claims](../tutorials/organise-with-tags.md) write into this crate's tag store.

## id-sync — 21 public modules

Purpose (from its `Cargo.toml`): "Sync layer of the id crate: log sync, the meta protocol, file operations and the meta client wire half (pkgs/id crate split step 3, #4406)". Step 3c added the friend-envelope and artifact family (`envelope`, `envelope_net`, `envelope_outbox`, `artifact`), seamed to worlds and the directory through `host::SessionHost` and `artifact::ArtifactSink`.

Public modules: `announcement`, `artifact`, `bridge`, `bridge_http`, `chained_message`, `dht`, `envelope`, `envelope_net`, `envelope_outbox`, `fanout_answer`, `federation` (the module plus `record_join`, `record_store`, `exchange_net`, and slices b01 to b32), `fileops`, `graph_frontier`, `host`, `log_sync`, `meta_client`, `protocol`, `registry`, `relay_gate`, `relay_ingest`, and `transfer`. One further module, `hex`, is `pub(crate)`.

Key types and functions: `protocol::META_ALPN = b"/iroh-meta/3"` and the `MetaRequest` / `MetaResponse` wire types; `log_sync` (p2panda log-sync sessions and the range-sync bridge; `SyncProtocol`); `transfer` (server-to-server file transfer, #5345, re-exported by the facade at `id::transfer` and exercised by [Tutorial: store, share and fetch a file between two nodes](../tutorials/share-a-file.md)); `artifact` (signed artifacts, push and pull); `envelope_outbox::Outbox` (an append-only journal, replayed on open); `announcement::{Announcement, SignedAnnouncement}` (sign and verify); `dht` (the opt-in mainline lookup policy); and the `federation` family (the #9106 federation exchange).

One naming trap is documented in the survey and worth repeating: this crate's `protocol` module is the wire half of the meta protocol. The facade-facing `MetaProtocol`, `FindMatch`, and `TaggedMatch` surface lives in `id-cli`'s `protocol` module, not here. A second disagreement sits in this crate's own `lib.rs`, which states that the `MetaClient` enum composition stays in the facade until step 7. Step 7b (#4411) has landed: `pub enum MetaClient` is defined at `crates/id-cli/src/meta_client.rs:31`, and the facade only re-exports the `meta_client` module from `id-cli` (disagreement LIB-10). The code behaviour is that `MetaClient` — the enum that chooses between a running server and the in-process local node — lives in `id-cli`.

Features: `default = []`; `dht = ["dep:iroh-mainline-address-lookup"]`, off by default, with the facade forwarding its own `dht` feature here; `test-utils = []`. Dependents: the facade, `id-cli`, `id-directory`, `id-web`, and `id-worlds`.

Embedding example: `id-cli`'s serve code wires this crate's surfaces into the running node, and the facade re-exports `transfer` at `id::transfer`; the `id send` command in [Tutorial: store, share and fetch a file between two nodes](../tutorials/share-a-file.md) is the user-visible end of that module.

## id-directory — 15 public modules

Purpose (from its `Cargo.toml`): "Directory layer of the id crate: home-server directory, views, auth, mail, invites and seitan admission (pkgs/id crate split step 6b, #4409)". It depends downward on `id-core`, `id-caps`, `id-events`, and `id-sync`, and never on `id-worlds`; the dependency runs the other way because `WorldCore` holds a `directory::Directory` as persisted state. The directory cluster used to live in `id-worlds`, which now re-exports these modules from this crate (disagreement LIB-8, covered in [Worlds and capabilities as a library](worlds-and-caps.md)).

Public modules: `directory` (with `directory/replica` and `directory/validate`), `directory_auth`, `directory_log`, `directory_mail`, `directory_view`, `invite`, `invites` (with `event` and `invitation`), `keyset`, `keyset_file`, `recovery`, `recovery_flow`, `recovery_relay`, `recovery_view`, `restored_key`, and `seitan` (with `accept`, `admit`, `derive`, `freshness`, and `token`).

Key types and functions: `Directory` (`new`, `open`, `replay`, `replay_read_only`, `append`, `sign_up`, `sign_up_keyed`, `add_key`, `remove_key`, `confirm_email`, `account_for`, `verify_account`), `Account`, `Group`, `Member`, `DirectoryEntry`, `Actor`, and the `BoundaryGate` trait; the seitan V2 admission protocol (HMAC-SHA512 invite derivation with a scrypt stretch, per the facade `Cargo.toml` comments); and the keyset and recovery family (`keyset`, `keyset_file`, `recovery`, `recovery_flow`, `recovery_relay`, `recovery_view`, `restored_key`).

Features: `default = []` only. Dependents: the facade, `id-cli`, `id-web`, and `id-worlds`.

Embedding example: `id-worlds` consumes this crate as persisted world state — `WorldCore` holds a `directory::Directory` — and re-exports `directory`, `directory_auth`, `directory_mail`, and `directory_view` so moved code keeps its historical paths. The web layer consumes the same crate for its directory routes (see [The web UI: pages and HTTP API](../reference/web-ui.md)).

## id-groups — 3 public modules

Purpose (from its `Cargo.toml`): "Groups layer of the id crate: group actions and log, local warnings, blob gate and group encryption (pkgs/id crate split step 4, #4407)". Its `lib.rs` is explicit about the boundary: the group model does not live here. Model, fold, policy, friendship, and writer-set vocabulary are owned by `id-events`; this crate is the policy and enforcement remainder over that model.

Public modules: `blob_gate`; `encryption` (with submodules `history`, `notice`, `ordering`, `roster`, `scheme`, `welcome`); and `groups` (with submodules `actions`, `log`, `warnings`), which re-exports the `id_events::groups` model at the same paths.

Key types and functions: `blob_gate::{BlobPolicy, Request, decide, decide_with_blocks, decide_with_local, blob_events, blob_events_with_blocks}` — the decision of who may fetch or observe a blob, taken over the `WorldAccess` trait from `id-events`; `encryption` — group encryption on p2panda-encryption's `DataScheme`, with a session adapter, causal ordering, history modes (`HistoryMode`, `HistoryRequest`, `HistoryAnswer`), and welcome chunking bounded by `id_core::frames::MAX_FRAME_BYTES`; and `groups::actions` (user group actions), `groups::log`, and `groups::warnings` (local size and churn warnings).

This crate's `lib.rs` carries the stale `FoldedWorlds` sentence recorded as disagreement LIB-9: it parks `FoldedWorlds` in the facade pending step 5, but step 5 has landed and the type is defined at `crates/id-worlds/src/blob_gate.rs:36`. The code behaviour is that `id-groups` owns the gate policy (`BlobPolicy`, `Request`, `decide`) and `id-worlds` owns the `FoldedWorlds` implementation of the gate's world trait.

Features: `default = []` only. Dependents: the facade, `id-cli`, `id-web`, and `id-worlds`.

Embedding example: command code reaches the gate through the `id-cli` bridge module, which re-exports `id_groups::blob_gate::{BlobPolicy, Request, decide}` alongside `id_worlds::blob_gate::{FoldedWorlds, RefreshingWorlds}` — one import site, two owner crates, matching the policy/implementation split above.
