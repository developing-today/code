# The crates

Source: primary sources are [pkgs/id/Cargo.toml](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/main/pkgs/id/Cargo.toml), [pkgs/id/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/main/pkgs/id/src/lib.rs), and the per-crate manifests and `lib.rs` files under [pkgs/id/crates/](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/main/pkgs/id/crates/). This chapter is complete without following those links; they record provenance only.

The `id` code lives in one Cargo workspace rooted at `pkgs/id/`. The workspace has 24 members: the root package `id` plus 23 crates under `pkgs/id/crates/`. Default members are 18: the root plus 17 crates. `id-native-shell`, `id-hw-shell`, `id-tui-shell`, `id-android-remote`, `id-bevy-bridge`, and `id-bevy-presenter` are workspace members but not default members, so a plain `cargo build` in `pkgs/id/` never acquires the Bevy desktop dependencies or the shell code. The 23 sub-crates declare 313 `pub mod` items in total across their `lib.rs` files; the root adds 26 modules of its own code plus about 90 re-exported modules (see [The id facade crate](facade.md)).

The workspace is the product of a numbered split of one original crate, recorded in the crate doc headers as umbrella issue #3447: Phase 1 extracted `id-core` (#3447), step 1 extracted `id-caps` (#4403), steps 2a and 2b extracted `id-store` and `id-events` (#4405), step 3 extracted `id-sync` (#4406), step 4 extracted `id-groups` (#4407), step 5 extracted `id-worlds` (#4408), steps 6b and 6c extracted `id-directory` and `id-web` (#4409), and step 7b extracted `id-cli` (#4411). Four crates are not split products: `id-config` (slice 9111-01, #10178), `id-currency-registry` (F1 and F2 of #2062, #2317 and #2318), `id-render` (#7918, decision records #5849 and #5855), and `id-import-notion` (slice 1, #8419); `id-client` (#13403), `id-tui-shell` (#12818), `id-pg` (#13760), and `id-grants` (#14790) are likewise purpose-built rather than split products. The Bevy pair — `id-bevy-bridge` (slices 1–3, #13702–#13704) and `id-bevy-presenter` (slices 4–5, #13705–#13706) — and `id-android-remote` (#1831) are purpose-built as well. The three shells are separate consumers, covered in [Guest apps and shells as consumers](guests-and-shells.md).

## Dependency direction

Production `[dependencies]` edges only; dev-dependencies are named where they matter.

- `id-core` has 10 production dependents: every other split crate plus the facade.
- `id-caps` depends on `id-core`. `id-store` depends on `id-core`. `id-events` depends on `id-core` and `id-store`. `id-groups` depends on `id-core`, `id-events`, and `id-store`. `id-sync` depends on `id-core`, `id-events`, and `id-store`.
- `id-directory` depends on `id-caps`, `id-core`, `id-events`, and `id-sync`. It does not depend on `id-worlds`; `id-worlds` depends on `id-directory`, because `WorldCore` holds a `directory::Directory` as persisted state.
- `id-worlds` depends on `id-caps`, `id-core`, `id-directory`, `id-events`, `id-groups`, `id-store`, and `id-sync`. `id-web` depends on all of those plus `id-worlds`. `id-cli` depends on the same set as `id-worlds`, plus optional `id-web`.
- The facade `id` depends on `id-caps`, `id-cli`, `id-core`, `id-currency-registry`, `id-directory`, `id-events`, `id-groups`, `id-store`, `id-sync`, and `id-worlds`, plus optional `id-web`.
- Both shells production-depend on the facade only, each with `default-features = false, features = ["world"]`. Each shell also dev-depends on `id-worlds` with the `sandbox` feature for its test harness.
- `id-config` has no production dependent anywhere in the workspace. The facade names it only under `[dev-dependencies]`, for the invariant tests in `pkgs/id/tests/`. ARCHITECTURE.md formerly stated that only the root depends on `id-config`; the code disagrees — that root edge is a dev-dependency, not a production dependency (disagreements LIB-4 and DES-11, now reconciled in ARCHITECTURE.md, which names the edge as dev-only).
- `id-currency-registry` depends on nothing and its only production dependent is the facade.
- `id-render` and `id-import-notion` depend on nothing and have no dependents, but both are default members and both carry maintained integration test suites. ARCHITECTURE.md calls them candidates for removal or for documenting as unused; no code, manifest, or test marks either crate deprecated or slated for removal (disagreement LIB-11).

**`id-grants` — 2 public modules.** Purpose: the selective-sharing grants model core (decision record #5799; slice 1 of #14790): a pure library with no I/O that validates grants against the model's invariants and answers whether a requester may perform an operation on an object. The surface is re-exported at the crate root: the grant (`Grant`, `GrantId`, `Subject`, `Scope`, `Operation`, `Duration`, `Delegation`), the set (`GrantSet`, with root and attenuated delegated inserts, revocation that cascades to children, and one-time snapshot consumption), the checks (`check_attenuation`, `AccessRequest`, `Requester`, `Decision`), the error vocabulary (`GrantError`), and `MAX_DELEGATION_DEPTH`. Public grants carry read and subscribe only and are never delegable (#5802). The `publication` module (#14781) holds the owner's publish and unpublish actions for public shares: `Publications` creates the public grant and its `PublicationRecord` together, derives the `ShareManifest` from the live record, mints unguessable `ShareId`s that are never reused, resolves an unpublished identifier exactly like an unknown one, and gates objects by a `ClassLevel` ceiling. Its `listing` (#14782) is the owner's public catalogue: `ListingEntry` values for live, listed shares only, re-checked against each share's grant at read time, with no counts. The `persist` module (#14790 slice 2b) gives a set a versioned stored form, `GrantSetSnapshot`, written by `GrantSet::to_snapshot`; `GrantSet::from_snapshot` re-validates every grant and every delegation's attenuation on load and refuses the whole snapshot with a `LoadError` if any check fails. Features: none. Dependents: `id-store`, whose `grant_authority` module adds the owner's live grants at the object tier of the #5678 read order; the remaining public-share slices (#14781 to #14784) build on it.

**`id-android-remote` — 0 public modules.** Purpose: the Android remote-drive transport bridge (#1831), in its `Cargo.toml`'s words: "Iroh session bridge for the Android remote-drive proof of concept (#1831)". The whole crate is one `src/lib.rs`; it speaks the existing `/id-world/1` shell session protocol and stays a client — it never starts a world host or listener. Features: none. Dependents: none; it is a workspace member but not a default member.

**`id-bevy-bridge` — 7 public modules.** Purpose: the Bevy projection bridge for id worlds, slices 1–3 (#13702, #13703, #13704). The journal is the only authority; Bevy holds a materialised projection. Public modules: `fixture`, `intent` (intents sent to the append gate, confirmed only when their journal position applies), `map` (`IdEntityMap`: ID entity id to Bevy `Entity`, plus the journal position at which each mapping was created), `oracle` (the replay oracle), `projection` (one journal position applied wholly or not at all), `schema` (`BridgeSchema`: predicate to component type, as data; classification is total and fail-closed), and `tail` (the atomic journal tail in `JournalSet`, with a per-tick lag budget). Features: none. Dependents: `id-bevy-presenter`; not a default member.

**`id-bevy-presenter` — 6 public modules.** Purpose: the Bevy presenter-client reference app for id worlds, Bevy slice 4 (#13705) with the host posture of slice 5 (#13706): a 2D app that joins a world hosted in another process, declares its provider manifest, and shows predicted presentation that reconciles or snaps back. Public modules: `app`, `host`, `journal_store`, `provider`, `remote`, and `world_host`. Features: none. Dependents: none; not a default member.

## Embedding examples from real call sites

The shipped binary is itself a facade consumer: `pkgs/id/src/main.rs:10-13` imports `id::{Cli, Command, PeekOptions, PeersOptions, SearchOptions, ServeOptions, cmd_find, cmd_get_multi, cmd_gethash, cmd_id, cmd_list, cmd_migrate_tags, cmd_peek, cmd_peers, cmd_put_hash, cmd_put_multi, cmd_search, cmd_serve, cmd_show, cmd_tag, run_repl}`, and `pkgs/id/src/main.rs:202` calls `id::cli::normalize_args(std::env::args_os())`.

The integration suite is the largest consumer: `use id::` occurs 165 times under `pkgs/id/tests/`. One import block in `pkgs/id/tests/first_app_poc.rs:33-45` spans three crates entirely through facade paths — `id-store` (`chain`, `documents`, `tag_claims`), `id-events` (`event_store`, `idset`), and `id-core` (`waiting`).

Direct crate paths are also in use where the facade does not re-export a crate. `pkgs/id/src/currency/gateway_poc.rs:120-125` imports `id_currency_registry::{AccountCluster, AccountKind, AccountSpec, ClusterError, CreateOutcome, Factory, FactoryError, InMemoryStore, Registry, RegistryError, Store, SupplyPolicy}` directly, because the facade exposes nothing from `id-currency-registry` at `id::` paths. The three config test files under `pkgs/id/tests/` (`config_invariants.rs`, `config_precedence.rs`, `config_downgrade.rs`) consume `id_config::` directly for the same reason.
