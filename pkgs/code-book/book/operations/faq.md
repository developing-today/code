# FAQ

## Why does the project need a book about itself, on top of the per-system books?

Because no per-system book can answer the cross-system questions: what
matters next across the whole project, and how the project got here. The
merged list needs one home that is not any single system's roadmap, and the
retrospective needs one home that is not any single system's history. This
book is those two homes, plus a map of the rest.

## Where do I find the detail on a system?

In that system's book — this book's systems chapters are condensations and
say so. The book map in [Code Book](../index.md) links every book, its
sources and its programme tracker.

## The merged list and a system's own roadmap disagree. Which is right?

They answer different questions. A system's roadmap is that programme's own
workstream order and issue register. The merged list is the cross-project
ranking: where the system's items sit in the whole project's order, and
why. Where a status itself disagrees, the living tracker is right and both
chapters are stale until re-censused — see [Keeping this book
current](../development/keeping-this-book-current.md).

## Why does the retrospective stop being detailed before October 2026?

Because the evidence does. The mined sources — merged PRs, dated records,
daily notes, the stewardship log — cover 2026-10-19 to 2026-11-23 fully,
2026-09-28 to 2026-10-18 thinly, and 2026-03-20 to 2026-04-01 as design
records only. For 2026-04-02 to 2026-09-27 they contain nothing, and the
book records the gap instead of reconstructing it. [The story so
far](../retrospective/the-story-so-far.md) states the coverage in full.

## Why is the Project Management book's address not in the book map?

Because it is not final. The PM book's skeleton was reconciled on
2026-11-23 under tracker #13900, and its subpath in the combined Pages
deploy is not yet settled. The map links its tracker and its repo path
instead of printing an address that does not exist.

## An item I care about has no projected date. When will it happen?

The census projected dates only where the record held them, and 22 of the
30 merged-list items carry no timing in the record. The list prints none
for them rather than inventing one. The item's tracker is where a date will
appear first.

## How do I get something onto the merged list?

Through a programme tracker or a census: an item is typed, placed by the
tier logic in [How priorities work](../priorities/how-priorities-work.md),
and given a written placement rationale at entry. A direction that belongs
inside an existing item rides that item's line — as the multi-transport
goal rides item 3 — instead of adding a line.
