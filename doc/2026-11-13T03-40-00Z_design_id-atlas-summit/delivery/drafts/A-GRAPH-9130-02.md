## Problem and goal
Fence references with a deletion-generation tombstone and CAS against reference-index generation/retention; consume #9137 and #9084 semantics before physical shared-object removal.

## Version and design basis
Version: V3. Settled design: https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/main/doc/2026-11-13T03-40-00Z_design_id-atlas-summit/part-a-graph/versioned-design.md#9-9130--moderation-deletion-and-gc (section: **9. #9130 — moderation, deletion, and GC**).
Tier: **contingent**. Start trigger: Do not enable shared-object physical deletion until #9137 and #9084 contracts are available and the adapter has fenced atomic compare-and-delete.
Statement IDs: S5.48, S5.53
Enables: GC cannot race a fresh reference or let an older worker delete after a newer retain/reference generation.
