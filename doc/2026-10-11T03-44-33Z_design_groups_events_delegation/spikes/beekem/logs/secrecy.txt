## T1: c's replica is copied (compromise); c stays in the group
  after b's update, the copy derives the new root: true
  after c's own update, the copy derives the new root: false
  the copy derives the epoch-0 root (earlier than the compromise): true

## T2: a removes c; c's copy is then fed public ops
  b (a member) derives the post-removal root: true
  c, informed of its removal, derives a root: false
  c that ignores its removal, given a's later update: Some(OutOfOrderOperation)
  c's copy derives the pre-removal (epoch-0) root: true
