- **Parent:** [#2055](https://github.com/developing-today/code/issues/2055)
- **Criterion reviewed:** “Placement decisions that are Drew's are surfaced with the substance (options, ramifications, recommendation), not just a label.”
- **Result:** Satisfied by the reviewed placement framework in [task-placement framework v1](../2026-11-03T15-10-56Z_design_task-placement-framework-v1/2026-11-03T15-10-56Z_design_task-placement-framework-v1.md), including its v1.1 machine-placement amendment.

## Evidence

The framework presents the placement alternatives and the trade-offs rather than merely naming a preferred surface:

- §1 (O1–O7) lists the seven options, their cost pools, and their supervision owners.
- §2 gives an ordered decision procedure, including explicit conditions and “never use” cases for each rule.
- §3.3 prices the supervision tax alongside pool burn and works through a weekly Actions sweep and quota-probe example.
- §4 E4 states that genuinely Drew-owned escalations carry the substance: what is being decided, options, ramifications, and recommendation.
- §8 adds the machine dimension using the settled #2179 registry shape; the amendment is dated 2026-11-22 in the framework header.

The source’s §3.4 also makes three predictions falsifiable for the review round. This audit records the trace from #2055’s acceptance criterion to that source; it does not claim that the remaining #2055 criteria are complete or that the framework’s predictions have since been measured.
