## Scope and distinction

Create a pure offline scenario simulator for comparing a few synthetic placement choices under explicit quality, latency, allowed-location and plan-envelope constraints. Inputs include versioned synthetic meter rates and age/uncertainty, storage, sync, reads/writes, embedding, egress, local hardware, temporary export/restore/compaction headroom and measured-or-unknown quality/latency. Output itemizes units and computed ranges, infeasibility or unknown, and a reversible proposal only. No live pricing, provider bindings, autonomous movement/deletion, real corpus, credentials or external costs. Constraints are evaluated before cost: sensitivity, legal retention, rights and pinned heads are never overridden.

This is not #6061's churn × retention growth forecast, #6041's retention rule engine/dry-run reporter, #6058 embedding ledger, or #7969 live operations guardrails. Consume no runtime services. Routine retirement stays cold-before-retire; independently required source-rights purge is not delayed or converted to archival.

## Failing-first steps and oracle

1. Hand-calculate tiny scenarios in a documented unit ledger: e.g. two equal-quality choices with itemized metering, a faster feasible choice within envelope, a forbidden placement, missing price, and insufficient temporary space. Write expected outputs before simulator implementation.
2. Implement a simple independently specified reference calculation. Model/property tests check determinism, nonnegative input validation, unit-preserving sums, monotonic cost for increased nonnegative use at fixed prices, hard-policy infeasibility even at zero cost, unknown propagation for missing measurements, and temporary headroom. An intentionally cheapest-first selector must fail a fixture where it violates the speed/quality or policy constraints.
3. Emit proposal + assumptions/uncertainty; never execute it. Hysteresis and rollback are modeled as constraints/annotations only, not a control-plane decision.

**Hard prerequisites:** this merged design and local synthetic fixtures. **Soft ordering:** #6061 calibration, #6058 actual ledger, #6041 retention policy and #7969 headroom. Future integration needs observed meter units/prices and policy approvals and must be separately scoped; no claim of current prices or deployment.

**Candidate seam:** existing `pkgs/atlas-search/crates/atlas-core/tests/` for an isolated `placement_model.rs`; existing growth model above is a design, not an executable dependency. Proposed future command: `cargo test --manifest-path pkgs/atlas-search/Cargo.toml -p atlas-core --test placement_model`. Use integer fixed units or documented precise arithmetic, not mixed currency/bytes.

**Risks:** unmeasured quality or stale rates cannot justify a placement; zero retrieval is not evidence of zero value. This simulation does not authorize purge, replace legal erasure, or prove operational savings.
