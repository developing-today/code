## Baseline contract inventory

Links are relative to `doc/` via `../../`; line ranges denote the relevant section, not executable proof.

* **V1-D01 — search/service boundary.** The Nov 4 binary design records existing Python hybrid retrieval and proposes Rust binary/service, client ceilings, capability-gated temporary storage and parity gates: [binary design](../../2026-11-04T08-07-13Z_design_atlas_binary_service/2026-11-04T08-07-13Z_design_atlas_binary_service.md#L21-L40), [service](../../2026-11-04T08-07-13Z_design_atlas_binary_service/2026-11-04T08-07-13Z_design_atlas_binary_service.md#L174-L210), [auth](../../2026-11-04T08-07-13Z_design_atlas_binary_service/2026-11-04T08-07-13Z_design_atlas_binary_service.md#L278-L346), [parity](../../2026-11-04T08-07-13Z_design_atlas_binary_service/2026-11-04T08-07-13Z_design_atlas_binary_service.md#L492-L555). Initial deployment figures are historical, not a current service check.
* **V1-D02 — delta-only spans.** Rolling 64-byte weak hash, strong hash plus byte comparison, 128-byte interior threshold, boundary exception; output is reused ranges plus genuinely new literals, against the previous version. [offset matching §§2–4](../../2026-11-08T10-26-00Z_design_atlas_offset_matching/2026-11-08T10-26-00Z_design_atlas_offset_matching.md#L27-L114). The matcher is a design and depends on #6035's span contract; hash alone is not evidence of equality.
* **V1-D03 — score comparability and partial trace.** A landed decision mandates one Snowflake model **and pipeline** across tiers; no arbitrary cross-model score merge: [decision](../../2026-11-08T10-44-00Z_decision_atlas_cross_tier_vector_comparability/2026-11-08T10-44-00Z_decision_atlas_cross_tier_vector_comparability.md#L15-L33), [merge contract](../../2026-11-08T10-44-00Z_decision_atlas_cross_tier_vector_comparability/2026-11-08T10-44-00Z_decision_atlas_cross_tier_vector_comparability.md#L126-L163). Landed [`FusedEntry`](../../../pkgs/atlas-search/crates/atlas-core/src/fusion.rs#L48-L55) carries chunk ID, fused score and FTS/vector ranks **in memory**; it is not durable end-to-end per-stage trace or feedback. #6027 is open and its referenced `doc/2026-11-10T20-41-01Z_design_atlas_retrieval_stats_feedback.md` is absent from this checkout: it is not a landed v1 design. The fresh [issue audit ledger](05-follow-on-landscape.md) describes open #8245–#8249 capture, retention, aggregation, feedback and normalization; issue proposals do not prove those capabilities exist.
* **V1-D04 — versions, aliases, occurrences.** A stable file identity has immutable versions, rename lineage, and chunk-to-version occurrences rather than copying embedded chunks; source-specific lineage rules are explicit. [version object model](../../2026-11-08T10-26-00Z_design_atlas_content_versioning_retention/2026-11-08T10-26-00Z_design_atlas_content_versioning_retention.md#L38-L150). #3125 additionally requires every observed occurrence and CAS or `repo@commit:path` pinning, not deduplication that silently drops provenance: [issue snapshot](07-issue-snapshot.json#L90-L91). Historical source locators are not necessarily complete, and aliases must not be equated with authenticated identity without evidence.
* **V1-D05 — query resolution.** Newest is a view, tombstoned heads do not resurrect older versions, explicit history can be addressed, and traces must resolve exactly what was served: [version query contract](../../2026-11-08T10-26-00Z_design_atlas_content_versioning_retention/2026-11-08T10-26-00Z_design_atlas_content_versioning_retention.md#L228-L272). Authorization still applies to historical/alias/graph resolution.
* **V1-D06 — keep-all versus retirement.** #3125's keep-all rule and version policy require cold-before-retirement and a report before deletion; age, increment and access triggers are policy inputs, not automatic authority: [retention](../../2026-11-08T10-26-00Z_design_atlas_content_versioning_retention/2026-11-08T10-26-00Z_design_atlas_content_versioning_retention.md#L151-L227). PITR is whole-database disaster recovery, not version retention: [decision](../../2026-11-05T15-50-41Z_decision_atlas_pitr_vs_retention/2026-11-05T15-50-41Z_decision_atlas_pitr_vs_retention.md#L11-L39).
* **V1-D07 — operational tiers.** Staging DBs, sensitivity-partitioned core and aggregator are designed separately: [scaler architecture](../../2026-11-04T21-27-34Z_design_atlas_scaler_revision/2026-11-04T21-27-34Z_design_atlas_scaler_revision.md#L213-L297). [Operations](../../2026-11-09T05-12-00Z_design_atlas_ingestion_operations/2026-11-09T05-12-00Z_design_atlas_ingestion_operations.md#L17-L40) treats #6028, #6057 and #6059 as unresolved at its verification date; secret sources are held out of automated sweeps until safe local embedding, not sent to a hosted embedder.

## Conflicts, missing evidence and dependencies

1. “Every place ever” is an aspiration, not evidence of a complete corpus: #3125 records lost historical occurrences. Public resolution must not reveal forbidden occurrence existence, counts, paths or graph degree; authorized gaps should be explicit.
2. Keep-all / cold-before-retirement does not forbid a separate legally required deletion-propagation process. Neither financial pressure nor positive/negative feedback independently authorizes deletion.
3. Writer-set membership determines a configured projection, not read access, truth or source ownership. A closed #5545 does not prove an Atlas graph adapter or deployed authorization.
4. The issue's hot/warm/cold and historical D1 references are commission-level directions, not proof of current operational tiers. Provider budgets and deployment require live verification; historical Scaler figures are not current prices.
5. #6028/#6035 delta schema, #3125 backfill and source locators, #6027 trace/feedback, #6057 promotion gate, #6021 classification recurrence, #6024 legal policy and #6059 local secret embedding are separate dependencies. A landed specialist design or closed issue is not a runtime gate. Several issue statuses quoted in old records may have changed.
6. Query-stage score provenance, manifest, attestation and cold-shard manifest are distinct objects and must not be conflated. A shared batch key does not make an attestation signed, a score calibrated or a policy decision authorized.
7. Original S39–S40 and source inventory are unavailable here; ask the parent to recover them or explicitly retain this limitation in final synthesis.
8. **Reported secret boundary breach:** the fresh [#8479 issue](https://github.com/developing-today/code/issues/8479), inspected via parent `gh`, reports clean shard boundaries but 638 secret-bearing chunks in the production monolith and its local replica, plus pre-tiering unclassified snapshots. This is a reported scan result, not a scan run by this design session. The older claim that secret material exists *only* in two secret DBs is false outside the shard set on that evidence. Purge versus secret-tier reclassification remains undecided; remediation and verification are prerequisites for any horizon claiming this global boundary.
9. **Evaluation prerequisite:** #6046's golden-set design remains open and PR #7656 is unlanded. Open [#8062](https://github.com/developing-today/code/issues/8062) identifies a concrete seam: [`EvalData`](../../../pkgs/atlas-search/crates/atlas-core/src/eval.rs#L246-L254) requires supplied `runs`, but the proposed master schema does not specify generating fresh baseline/candidate rankings. The evaluator scores supplied rankings, not retrieval itself. No horizon may claim a working feedback-derived golden gate until this seam and the actual gate are implemented and verified. See the [issue audit ledger](05-follow-on-landscape.md).

## Repository runtime evidence (bounded inspection)

The checked-in staging schema stores raw text and sensitivity labels but explicitly says sensitivity is not enforced there; raw chunks have text and hashes, not embedded vectors: [`atlas_stage_v1.sql:33–67`](../../../pkgs/atlas-search/schema/atlas_stage_v1.sql#L33-L67). Its generic cursor KV is not proof of provenance offsets: [schema:84–92](../../../pkgs/atlas-search/schema/atlas_stage_v1.sql#L84-L92). The staging implementation expressly excludes the aggregator/embedder, actual merge-on-close, physical sensitivity stores, session-close hook, and end-to-end demo: [`stage.rs:14–30`](../../../pkgs/atlas-search/crates/atlas-core/src/stage.rs#L14-L30). Its promotion path validates model metadata and changes state, not proof of an actual corpus merge: [`stage.rs:347–408`](../../../pkgs/atlas-search/crates/atlas-core/src/stage.rs#L347-L408). These observations are narrower than asserting no other implementation exists; no runtime or deployment was exercised. The auditor found no PII/redaction implementation in the Atlas Rust source, and pagination offsets must not be mistaken for delta/span provenance.

**Rehoming seam:** Atlas's [`crates/atlas-search/src/server.rs`](../../../pkgs/atlas-search/crates/atlas-search/src/server.rs#L1-L5) identifies its fleet counterpart [`pkgs/fleet/crates/fleet-search/src/server.rs`](../../../pkgs/fleet/crates/fleet-search/src/server.rs) and the parity guard [`.github/scripts/check-fleet-search-parity.sh`](../../../.github/scripts/check-fleet-search-parity.sh). The Atlas [`render.rs`](../../../pkgs/atlas-search/crates/atlas-search/src/render.rs#L1-L5) and [`pyjson.rs`](../../../pkgs/atlas-search/crates/atlas-search/src/pyjson.rs#L1-L5) likewise identify fleet copies [`render.rs`](../../../pkgs/fleet/crates/fleet-search/src/render.rs) and [`pyjson.rs`](../../../pkgs/fleet/crates/fleet-search/src/pyjson.rs); fleet's service entry is [`serve.rs`](../../../pkgs/fleet/crates/fleet-search/src/serve.rs). A future implementation must identify and validate both surfaces, not assume an Atlas-only edit reaches fleet.
