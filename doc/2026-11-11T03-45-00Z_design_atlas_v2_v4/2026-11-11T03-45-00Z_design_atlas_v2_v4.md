# Atlas beyond v1: evidence, adaptation and stewardship

**Design record · 2026-11-11 UTC · [#6020](https://github.com/developing-today/code/issues/6020)**

This is a capability roadmap, not an implementation, deployment claim, calendar or
authorization to migrate providers. It extends the landed v1 design contracts
without treating their existence as proof of running software. Its central rule is:
**establish what the authorized corpus contains and what the user actually saw
before learning to change ranking**. Each capability may advance independently once
its own entry gate holds; v2–v4 are not three simultaneous releases.

## 2. Architecture and identity

### Separate planes and three axes

The read plane authenticates a caller, obtains current effective authorization,
selects eligible physical stores, and only then generates, ranks and hydrates
candidates. The feedback/trace append plane is separately authorized; it cannot
mutate source history or policy. The aggregator remains the designated promotion
owner. Ingest and deletion authorities own their respective decisions. A model or
curation proposal cannot become a new core writer merely by producing output.

Classification/security, temperature and source/retention class are independent
axes. A hot secret session and a cold public document differ on all three. The
manifest declares non-authoritative filtering/storage metadata; neither its label,
writer set, similarity score nor popularity grants a principal access. Select
authorized partitions before ranking, and check again before expansion,
occurrence enumeration, hydration, cache reads, telemetry, export and replay.
Any stale or unverifiable authorization state fails closed. Historical snapshots
are evaluated against **current** authorization and lawful retention, not historical
permissions. Public absent and forbidden responses have the same non-revealing
shape, including counts, pagination and graph-degree disclosures.

### Identity and records

| Record            | Minimum distinct identity and metadata                                                                              | Invariant                                                                                               |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Content           | digest algorithm, canonical byte/representation definition, encoding and normalization revision                     | Hash is integrity evidence, not signer, author or rights proof; equality needs appropriate verification |
| Logical file      | source namespace and stable `file_id`, aliases and identity evidence                                                | Rename and copy are not automatically equivalent                                                        |
| Immutable version | `(file_id, version_seq)`, source revision, observed/effective time, representation                                  | Same bytes may occur in different versions                                                              |
| Occurrence        | occurrence ID, version, source-native locator, span, security domain and observation                                | Equal bytes do not merge occurrences, permissions or license rights                                     |
| Anchor            | version, offset unit and interval, representation/pipeline revision, permitted digest or context                    | Ambiguous remapping is not an exact citation                                                            |
| Evidence edge     | typed endpoints, asserting actor/method, evidence, valid/observed time, confidence, decision revision               | Similarity creates a candidate, not authoritative identity                                              |
| Exposure          | query/interaction key, snapshot, authorized scope, stage, positions, revisions and displayed/context-included state | Candidate is not necessarily seen                                                                       |
| Feedback          | event ID, actor/scope, exposure and version, judgment/reason, timestamp, correction or retraction link              | A vote is not permanent document truth                                                                  |
| Policy decision   | policy revision, decision time, scope and permitted reason                                                          | A historical decision cannot override current revocation                                                |

Record configuration and identity references separately from sensitive text.
Exact replay requires the actual retained bytes, configuration, compatible
artifacts and present permission. Otherwise produce an authorized unavailable or
tombstone response; never regenerate new text and present it as old evidence.
Even hashes and tombstone metadata may be sensitive and require policy review.

## 4. Seven threads

### 4.1 Feedback and retrieval [V2-F → V3-F → V4-F]

**First build the evaluation harness.** Given a query, intended claim, principal,
snapshot and independently known target, compare a complete fixture inventory to
ingest, index, candidate generation, fusion, reranking, cutoff, context packing
and answer/citation stages. Diagnostic output has two dimensions: stage and
coverage confidence. Distinguish unknown/incomplete coverage; known absent from
the enumerated eligible snapshot; present but unindexed; indexed but not a
candidate; candidate below cutoff; evidence delivered but answer unsupported;
and intentionally excluded by version, tier or policy where disclosure is
permitted. Forbidden targets get ordinary non-revealing responses. A source outage
or missing retained source is an ingestion question, not a ranker failure.

Positive-control fixture targets prove existence; negative controls enumerate the
entire eligible fixture. Production top-k alone cannot prove absence. A pooled
judgment set samples independently discovered sources, but unjudged remains
unknown. The trace includes snapshot and index/pipeline revisions, per-stage
candidate IDs and rank/score semantics, cutoff, inclusion and latency; its access
classification is at least as restrictive as its inputs. Raw queries and secret
snippets are not logged by default. Feedback points to a specific exposure and
version, has an actor, timestamp, reason and revocation/correction path, and is
kept only under its own authorized retention policy. No click is not a negative;
answer-level disapproval is not automatically a relevance label. Duplicate copies
and agent self-citations are not independent human judgments.

V2 compares the deterministic existing baseline with lexical, exact dense and
hybrid alternatives on a frozen judgment set. V1's shared embedding model and
pipeline remain mandatory for cross-tier vector comparisons; score normalization
cannot repair incompatible vector spaces. ANN needs the same-snapshot exact scan
as neighbor oracle. V3 may use explicitly consented labels, time-separated and
duplicate-aware splits, and controlled shadow/online trials. It must avoid treating
selection-biased shown results as the complete corpus, and keep a no-personalization
baseline for sparse histories. V4 may test bounded exploration only with opt-in,
actual action/slate propensities, support/overlap, variance diagnostics and a
randomized holdout. A single-action estimator does not automatically estimate a
whole ranked slate. Insufficient evidence means abstention, not a causal claim.
Restore the full previous model, pipeline, index and cache configuration on a
guardrail breach.

#### Diagnostic contract and the fresh-ranking seam [V2-F; D01, D03, D04, D05]

The diagnostic harness has four independent inputs, not just a qrels file:

1. A **coverage inventory**: source scopes, expected and observed versions,
   committed ingestion watermarks, indexing state and intentional exclusions.
2. A **judgment set**: task/query, intended claim, relevant occurrence/version
   IDs, graded assessment and assessor provenance. `unknown` is not grade zero.
3. A **fresh run bundle** for each baseline/candidate configuration: corpus and
   index snapshot, query/model/pipeline identity, effective filters and policy,
   candidate-stage records, served ranking, context-packing record and errors.
4. An **independent oracle**: exhaustive fixture truth for diagnosis; human
   judgments with documented discovery limits for real relevance comparisons.

The existing `atlas-core/src/eval.rs` consumes supplied rankings. A future runner
must execute both configurations, persist their run bundles, validate all joins,
then feed compatible `runs` to that evaluator. Compilation of judgments alone
cannot produce a retrieval comparison. Reject a stale run, missing query,
duplicate key, mismatched snapshot, missing policy context or wrong pipeline;
do not fill a missing ranking with an empty list and call it a real miss.
Fixture work can inject these run bundles now without live Atlas access.

| Fixture truth                                    | Stage observation                   | Correct privileged diagnostic                                |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Source coverage incomplete                       | No target found anywhere            | `unknown/coverage-incomplete`, not corpus absent             |
| Fully enumerated eligible inventory lacks target | No indexed/candidate record         | `absent-in-declared-scope`                                   |
| Eligible stored version exists                   | Index watermark has not included it | `present/index-not-ready`                                    |
| Eligible indexed target exists                   | Neither branch generated it         | `present/candidate-miss`                                     |
| Candidate target exists at rank 12               | Served cutoff is 10                 | `present/rank-cutoff`                                        |
| Target survives top-K                            | Token packing omitted it            | `present/context-budget`                                     |
| Supporting span delivered                        | Generated claim misquotes it        | `served/answer-support-failure`                              |
| Target exists only outside current authority     | Any internal observation            | Ordinary caller receives the same not-found shape as absence |

The ranks above are a constructed example, not a measurement. Several relevant
targets may fail at different stages; diagnosis is per target and can have several
causes per query. A timeout is not an empty candidate set. A cold target excluded
by the requested search mode is neither proof of global absence nor a rank miss.

Trace persistence failure needs an explicit contract with #6027, not a quiet
best-effort logger. Its proposed #8245 slice requires durable trace before serving;
the exact failure/availability trade-off remains that issue's to settle. This
design cannot advertise complete exposure capture if untraced successes are
possible. A configured, explicitly disclosed baseline mode without diagnostic
claims is different from silently losing required records.

#### Ranking options and experimental progression [V2-F, V3-F, V4-F; D01, D03, D09]

| Option                                             | When attractive                                             | Costs and disqualifiers                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Keep current lexical/dense baseline                | Sparse or absent trustworthy feedback; easy reproducibility | Does not adapt to individual tasks; measure failures rather than assume it suffices          |
| Tune global hybrid weights                         | Small, reviewable parameter space; explicit labels          | Can overfit a small golden set; weights do not fix missing ingestion or incompatible vectors |
| Pairwise/listwise learned reranker                 | Sufficient scoped judgments and hard negatives              | Feature/label leakage, latency, deployment artifacts and retraining/deletion obligations     |
| Per-user adapters with pooled priors               | Stable individual differences and consent                   | Cold start, small samples, privacy, forgetting and minority-task regressions                 |
| Contextual policy selecting search depth or ranker | V3 evidence shows workload differences worth exploiting     | Requires bounded actions, randomized support and honest cost/reward logging                  |
| LLM-expanded queries or graph summaries            | A measured class of global/ambiguous questions              | Additional cost, prompt injection, hallucinated links and weaker source-level fidelity       |

None is selected as a universal successor. Use a time-split holdout and a frozen
regression pack; partition near-duplicate versions together. Report per-query
deltas and uncertainty, not only a global average. Answer quality, citation
support and retrieval quality have separate judges. An LLM judge can prioritize
human review but is not the sole oracle. An experiment whose confidence is too
weak to justify a change is an inconclusive result, not a release gate passed.

An experimental artifact bundle names feature definitions, label/consent snapshot,
training recipe, model, index generation, baseline configuration and cache-key
revision. A kill switch restores a compatible whole bundle. If old source rights
have been revoked, rollback may restore its configuration but cannot restore
forbidden training data or serving bytes; disable the learned artifact until a
compliant one exists. Retractions become explicit dataset exclusions and a
retraining/replacement requirement where the model may retain their influence.

### 4.2 Provenance-complete resolution [V2-P → V3-P → V4-P]

Resolve a result through content → occurrence → immutable version → source-native
location. Capture source namespace, revision or commit, path, exact byte/line
representation and interpretation revision. The same content can have many
occurrences with distinct permissions, authors and licenses. Version pins do not
silently float to newer content, and a tombstoned head does not resurrect an older
head. A citation may show several _permitted_ paths, not an all-world claim.

Resolver response states are complete-for-declared-snapshot, partial coverage,
stale, unavailable, ambiguous and deleted, subject to disclosure policy. Coverage
includes source inventory, ingestion watermark, retained state, graph revision and
authorized scope; an outage or unobserved source prevents an absence assertion.
Check an anchor against pinned bytes before rendering. Position and quote selectors
have different failure modes: normalization, OCR and repeated text may create
ambiguous mappings; return uncertainty instead of silently choosing. Do not retain
quote text in an unrestricted log to make replay convenient.

V3 adds evidence-qualified links between occurrences and transformations, including
conflicting interpretations. V4 can export portable evidence receipts only after
policy and signature verification requirements are settled. A hash verifies
integrity under stated assumptions; it is not a signature, signer identity,
permission or proof that the statement is true. If retention or current access no
longer permits bytes, historical replay is an honest unavailable response.

#### Resolver protocol, independent of storage engine [V2-P, V3-P; D02, D04, D05, D10, D14]

A resolution request supplies a content/occurrence/version reference, desired
historical state, allowed detail level and byte/time budget. The authenticated
server derives current rights; a caller-supplied ceiling is only a narrowing
request. A resolution receipt binds the returned references to the corpus,
representation, lineage interpretation and policy revisions used. A continuation
is opaque and tied to that same scope; it cannot enumerate another caller's
occurrences or keep working across revocation.

The resolver's conceptual order is:

```text
authenticate and narrow scope
  locate permitted occurrence/version references
  check current suppression, purge and retention state
  resolve pinned representation and source coordinates
  verify selected bytes and mapping
  enumerate permitted alternate occurrences within budget
  return evidence + coverage/revision receipt, or honest unavailable
```

The receipt is a contract sketch, not a new wire-schema commitment. It must
distinguish `bytes verified`, `source reachable`, `lineage inferred` and `author
authenticated`: none implies the others. Source lines are a rendering of a
specified byte representation; normalization or reflow cannot silently change
the cited interval. Delta matching's reusable copy spans do not establish
cross-source authorship, and partial spans do not inherit a whole span's embedding.

**Constructed lifecycle example.** File A at commit c1 and session B at revision
b7 contain the same bytes. A at c2 renames and edits the paragraph. Store the
shared bytes once _within a permitted storage domain_, with separate A/c1 and
B/b7 occurrences; A/c2 has its own immutable version and evidenced predecessor.
An A/c1 pin never returns A/c2 merely because it is now the head. Deletion of B
removes B's serving eligibility, but does not erase A if A independently remains
authorized and lawful. Conversely, B's continued existence cannot preserve an
unlawful A occurrence or disclose A's path. Reference accounting and purge policy
must operate on occurrences and derived stores, not blindly delete shared bytes
or blindly keep every copy. If the policy requires stronger separation, duplicate
physical storage is the correct cost of that boundary.

Cold restore verifies the shard and version bytes, then rechecks current rights
and suppression before serving or promoting. Pack-time permissions and retention
metadata are historical observations, not current authority. The cold catalog's
integrity hash must be obtained from a trusted catalog or verified policy-bound
receipt if authenticity is needed; an attacker can replace bytes _and_ their
untrusted checksum. This is a verifier requirement, not an assertion that v1's
checksum-only container is already signed.

### 4.3 Cross-source lineage [V2-G → V3-G → V4-G]

Files, chunks, source revisions, sessions, issues, notes, agent outputs and
ingestion runs can be distinct typed nodes. Edges such as cites, derived-from,
observed-in, supersedes, contradicts, alias and possible-match carry evidence,
asserting actor/method, valid time and revision. An ingestion run's declared input
does not prove every output span derives from every input. Writer-set projection
answers whose writes count in a specified view; it does not answer who may read,
who owns a source, or whether an assertion is true.

Start with direct source-native references and typed relational edges. A candidate
link produced by embedding similarity stays provisional; no destructive global
same-as closure. Versioned curation decisions can accept, reject, split or reverse
links with compare-version conflict handling, while source evidence remains intact
where lawful. Each query traverses only authorized nodes and edges, with limits on
depth, fan-out, bytes and time. Continuations bind to snapshot and authorization;
revocation invalidates them. A derived graph index must have a watermark, replay,
reconciliation and a fail-closed lag policy. A separate graph engine is a measured
option, not a required rewrite or source of authority. Dense all-path traversal may
be exponential even when ordinary adjacency is linear in edges.

#### Projection and authority seam [V2-G, V3-G, V4-G; D04, D11, D12, D14]

Keep Atlas's occurrence/version records canonical for this design; an ID-graph
publication or search graph is a rebuildable projection. #6062 owns the actual
publication mapping, so this is an integration constraint, not its replacement.
Graph events distinguish `observed`, `candidate`, `accepted`, `rejected`,
`superseded` and `revoked`. Deleting a projected edge is not deletion of source
evidence; a rights purge may require both and every materialized summary that
copied it. Cycles are permitted for citations or disagreement but must not turn
version ancestry into an invalid cycle.

The writer-set view needs its full input tuple: view configuration, set version,
claim frontier and resolution inputs. Its rule is **stored does not mean counted**;
counted does not mean readable or true. The [source audit](research/03-provenance-lineage.md)
also identifies a current-group-state versus historical-frontier limitation in
the implementation. A named tuple alone cannot certify historical membership.
Until a historical implementation or a verifiable frozen expansion is proven,
show attributed source claims without claiming a reproduced historical authority
view. Do not interpret a closed #5545 as that missing proof.

Relational adjacency is the lowest-dependency option for exact source links;
PROV is an interchange vocabulary, OpenLineage describes pipeline runs, and a
graph engine offers traversal/index facilities. These solve different problems.
An optional graph backend must demonstrate enough marginal evidence retrieval to
pay for its duplicate storage, projection lag, rebuild, access and purge surface.
Community summaries are derived content with support paths, not substitute
provenance. A contradiction view preserves competing claims and their time scopes
instead of deciding truth from link count or repeated copies.

### 4.4 Self-curation [V2-C → V3-C → V4-C]

Usefulness is evidence for a proposal, never an authority to erase, reclassify or
override a source right. A version not indexed, exposed or fairly ranked has no
meaningful non-use signal. Model exposure, coverage, query class, confidence,
age, source/retention class, dependency links and pinned heads before proposing a
reversible placement change or a retention review. Include competing policies,
reason and evidence window, expected headroom, uncertain cost and a rollback
target. Human review sees only authorized evidence.

Transitions are proposed → approved/rejected → applied → verified → reverted,
with stale/conflicting proposals re-evaluated against the current policy version.
Use hysteresis to avoid oscillation. V3 can rank proposals only after adequate
exposure coverage; V4 may delegate narrowly scoped reversible actions after audit.
Usefulness-driven irreversible deletion always requires human approval and policy
authorization. Routine retirement remains cold-before-retire under V1-D06.
Required privacy or legal purge is a separate authority-driven path, with **no
archive-first delay**. A failed purge must not be masked as successful curation.

#### Proposal record and state-machine boundary [V2-C, V3-C, V4-C; D06, D12, D13]

Minimum proposal evidence comprises target versions/occurrences, current and
proposed placement, purpose, policy revision, measurement window and coverage,
exposure-qualified usefulness, pins/dependents, estimated bytes/cost/latency,
uncertainty, expiration, approver and rollback target. Approval binds that exact
proposal revision; an intervening head change, rights change or policy update
invalidates approval rather than extending it to a different object.

| Event                       | Preconditions                                                             | Next state / failure                                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Propose                     | Measurements and scope available                                          | Pending; no storage mutation                                             |
| Approve                     | Authorized approver, unchanged evidence/policy, bounded reversible action | Approved revision                                                        |
| Apply                       | Approval current; capacity reserved; source still eligible                | Applying; retries use the same operation identity                        |
| Verify                      | Destination checksum/identity and current rights pass                     | Applied; source removal only under the owning mover contract             |
| Conflict / expired approval | Any bound input changed                                                   | Stale; re-evaluate, never silently broaden approval                      |
| Reverse                     | Lawful retained source/rollback target still exists                       | Reverted; otherwise unavailable, not reconstructed fiction               |
| Rights withdrawal           | Confirmed policy signal                                                   | Separate purge workflow; cancel incompatible proposals and fence serving |

This is the reference model for later MBT, not a chosen database schema.
Compare least-recently-used, least-frequently-used, exposure-normalized usefulness,
source-class rules and owner pins on the same held-out workload. Frequency is
simple but reinforces popularity; recency adapts quickly but thrashes; learned
utility can improve prediction but hides uncertainty. Preserve rare-query and
historical-research cohorts. Do not train usefulness on traffic caused by a prior
policy's own visibility decisions and call the result unbiased demand.

### 4.5 Classification-aware retrieval [V2-S → V3-S → V4-S]

For each request, derive a current principal, permitted stores and policy revision;
choose partitions before any candidate generation or score aggregation. Apply
policy again to every expansion, occurrence, hydration, cached result, trace,
export and replay. Cross-tier content dedup does not mean shared rights. A secret
chunk must never hydrate an internal context, including through a graph neighbor,
summary, feedback feature or cache. Do not infer physical-store mapping from the
older logical ladder; explicitly decide and migrate it with tests.

Secret source ingestion remains gated on safe local embedding per the operational
design. [#8479](https://github.com/developing-today/code/issues/8479) reports that
legacy monolith/local replicas can still hold secret labels despite clean shard
boundaries. The V2 safety gate must include those copies and their read paths, or
exclude them by effective credentials until remediated; shard-only tests do not
prove the boundary. Invalid manifests or stale revocation information fail loudly without
widening. Noninterference tests compare ordinary responses with and without hidden
records, including counts and timing-sensitive pagination where feasible. A
privileged auditor may see policy-permitted diagnostics, but that interface is not
an excuse to leak forbidden existence to the ordinary caller.

#### Cache, feature and explanation boundaries [V2-S, V3-S, V4-S; D01, D07, D08, D12]

A result-cache identity must include the effective query/filter surface, corpus
and model/pipeline generation, principal or proven-equivalent access domain,
authorization/revocation revision, version mode and any view/graph/ranker revision
that affects the result. A matching ceiling alone is not proof of identical
rights. Recheck current serving eligibility at read time; immutable cache bytes
do not make permission immutable. Query vectors and raw queries can themselves
reveal sensitive intent, so the scaler's shared query cache is not automatically
appropriate across principals or sensitivity domains.

Lower-authority ranking features, explanations, embeddings or graph summaries
must not be trained or computed from hidden higher-tier records and then exposed
as if filtering only the final text were enough. The first implementation uses
separate authority-compatible artifacts or excludes those features. Measuring
safe aggregate/declassified statistics is a later policy decision, not an
exception invented by a ranker. Diagnostic logs need restricted storage and an
audit-purpose reader; public telemetry exposes neither hidden counts nor policy
reasons that reveal forbidden sources.

Strict timing noninterference is not proven by equal response JSON. Differential
fixtures first check deterministic outputs, counts and cursors; adversarial
workload measurements separately assess timing, error, cache-hit and resource
side channels. Do not claim constant-time retrieval. Physical partitioning and
principal-scoped caches reduce the surface; a residual channel needs an explicit
threat-model disposition rather than a universal zero-leak assertion.

### 4.6 Cost-aware tiering [V2-T → V3-T → V4-T]

The governing choice is speed-first within a plan envelope, not minimum dollars
at any cost. Turso, D1 and rustfs are existing _roles_, not a requirement to copy
every object into each provider, and D1 is not cross-tier corpus authority.
Temperature, sensitivity and source class remain separate. Default query fan-out
excludes cold; a caller explicitly requesting retained cold history gets a
separately authorized, bounded restore/open path with timeout and partial state.
Unavailability does not license returning a different version as the requested one.

Account separately for storage bytes, temporary restore/export/compaction space,
duplicate bytes, sync, reads/writes, embedding tokens, egress and local hardware.
Record meter units, measured period, price age, uncertainty and policy version.
Forecast growth from measured churn and retention rather than pretending historical
numbers are current prices. A workload comparison measures recall, tail latency,
freshness and restoration cost together; rare and historical queries must not
disappear inside average savings. Missing metrics mean static conservative
placement, not automatic deletion or optimistic capacity assumptions.

#### Placement objective and simulation seam [V2-T, V3-T, V4-T; D06, D07, D09, D13, D15]

"Warm" is an optional configured replica/index residency class, not a fourth
security tier or a claim that another production store exists. Candidate policies
include static source-class placement, bounded hot working sets, read-through
authorized replicas and measured predictive placement. Compare them before adding
a controller; a static policy may be the best answer at the present scale.

For each meter, preserve its units and reset window. Conceptually:

```text
predicted storage peak = retained bytes + new batch + staging
                      + export/restore/compaction overlap + safety reserve
predicted spend = sum(usage[meter, window] * verified unit_price[meter, window])
```

The spending expression does not turn a storage allowance into dollars or mix
monthly sync with GB-month storage. Prices are versioned inputs, not hard-coded
2026-11-04 plan numbers; unknown values stay unknown. Local compute has time,
energy/capacity and contention costs even when the incremental cash bill is zero.
The growth model's byte-weighted reuse and the ledger's chunk-count reuse are
different statistics and cannot be substituted.

The objective is a **feasible quality/latency frontier**, subject to approved
plan limits, authorization, source rights, retention and recovery constraints.
Do not collapse those hard constraints into weights a learned optimizer may
trade away. Report each candidate's quality and tail-latency deltas, costs and
uncertainty; reject infeasible candidates before ordering the feasible set.

**Constructed storage check:** with 100 GiB usable capacity, 80 GiB retained,
8 GiB planned ingest and 15 GiB temporary export/restore overlap, peak is 103 GiB
before reserve. A final-state forecast of 88 GiB would incorrectly accept it.
The correct dry-run disposition is defer/reduce the batch or request an approved
capacity decision, not delete history or transiently exceed the ceiling. This
example tests arithmetic only; it is not Atlas's measured capacity or quota.

Cold objects use the v1 format, catalog and byte-identical round-trip oracle.
Routine cold-before-retirement and the operations guardrails still govern.
The controller cannot increase the plan, change a secret route, create a public
replica or convert a denial into a cold retrieval. If metering is stale, fall
back to conservative static placement and pause affected automatic growth under
the existing operations policy. Cost pressure never turns a partial query into
a complete answer or hides a paid overage.

### 4.7 Agent-facing corpus [V2-A → V3-A → V4-A]

V2 provides a read-only search/resolve interface: a request names task, principal,
scope, snapshot or history pin, token/time budgets and permitted source classes.
Response carries result/version/occurrence identity, exact citation anchor,
authorized source metadata, freshness, coverage and uncertainty. The agent treats
retrieved text as untrusted data, not instructions. It may cite only evidence it
actually received and can resolve; do not silently turn an agent hypothesis into
an authoritative human claim. No generic write-capable agent token is implied.

V3 adds bounded connected and contradictory evidence views, preserving provenance
and distinguishing human assertion, third-party quotation, system observation,
agent hypothesis and verified outcome. V4 may propose actions with narrow scopes,
explicit budgets, review and rollback, contingent on the C/S gates. A portable
evidence receipt, feedback export and coverage-repair request are experiments, not
mandatory mechanisms. Continuous capture is a separate opt-in source class with
consent, exclusions, retention and egress policy; a local capture process does not
by itself guarantee privacy, deletion or legal permission.

#### Producer-to-consumer arc [V2-A, V3-A, V4-A; D01, D02, D04, D07, D10, D12, D13, D14]

The producer path remains source-native and asynchronous: finalized session/chat/
report → delta discovery → class usefulness and rights checks → isolated staging
→ classify/dedup/provenance gate → attested promotion → indexed-readiness receipt.
An agent finishing work cannot self-publish unreviewed text directly into core or
declare its own authority. Record source session/run and actor, exact revision,
generated-versus-observed status and supported claim spans. Synthetic heartbeat
noise does not become memory simply because a session ended.

The consumer path retrieves evidence under the task's principal and egress policy.
An internal service token does not authorize sending internal snippets to an
external model; execution location and allowed disclosure are separate checks.
History of an agent's work is searchable by future agents **only when their
current scope permits it**. Source facts, user preferences, task state and agent
hypotheses are different memory classes with different expiry and authority.

An evidence receipt records which authorized spans actually entered context, not
every result that happened to rank. A final claim can name supporting and opposing
spans, freshness and uncertainty. Correct absence/unavailability is a successful
outcome when support is missing; generating an answer anyway does not earn recall.
The agent can request a scoped coverage investigation, but new source classes,
bulk backfills and rights decisions remain subject to v1 operational approvals.

Further experiments include a portable research packet, user-owned feedback
export, contradiction timeline and coverage-repair suggestions. They extend the
seven threads rather than require a new canonical memory service. Test a packet
after its producer is offline, its source changes and its permission is revoked;
portability without those lifecycle checks is only a file export.

## 6. Performance, operations and lifecycle

### Budgets and consistency

Every request has limits for fan-out, candidates, graph depth and edges, hydration
bytes, cold opens, elapsed time and explanation payload. Every training or
maintenance job has resource/cost budgets and cancellation. Budgets cannot weaken
v1's selected-shard completeness contract: **failure or timeout of any selected
fan-out shard fails the whole search loudly**, including a budget cancellation
mid-scan. Never merge surviving selected shards into a partial ranking. This
preserves `atlas-core/src/shards/scan.rs` and V1-D03's explicit failure posture.
An optional graph expansion, explanatory occurrence page or separately requested
cold restoration may report its own incomplete/unavailable state without
misrepresenting a successful base search. Any proposal for partial selected-shard
search requires a separately approved contract amendment and evaluation.
Set numerical limits after representative workload measurement;
any initial values are unmeasured defaults, not claimed SLOs. Capture p50/p95/p99,
throughput, queue age, recall, index lag, memory, disk, sync, re-embedding and
restore costs. Do not extrapolate linear speedup from old scaler arithmetic.

Typed adjacency plus indexes has O(V+E) base storage, excluding evidence and
replicas. Bounded traversal visits its reachable slice plus authorization and
sorting costs; all-path expansion can be exponential. Measure representative
high-degree nodes, concurrent revocation and rebuilds before selecting an index or
graph engine. The latter duplicates projections and adds lag, reconciliation and
purge obligations. Source records and policy decisions remain authoritative.

### Recovery, privacy and rights

Ingest events need stable IDs, idempotence, ordering rules, watermarks, dead-letter
handling and reconciliation; a successful durable write is not indexed readiness.
Snapshots explicitly mark pending, indexed, partial, quarantined and unavailable
states. Failed or stale authorization fences serving until checks recover.
Revocation invalidates caches, continuations, summaries, graph projections,
training exports and derived memories. Deletion propagation must cover vectors,
replicas, cold shards and backups according to the controlling policy; restore
replays revocations before serving. Retain only permitted audit receipts, since
even content hashes and existence metadata can reveal sensitive information.
External copies already delivered cannot necessarily be recalled.

License capture records observations and policy inputs, not a legal conclusion or
grant to use text for model training. Export, training, embedding, display and
retention may have different permitted purposes. If policy or rights are unknown,
do not widen use. Keep source rights, retention, consent and access separate from
model-generated labels. No trace, graph or feedback artifact is exempt from the
policy governing the source from which it was derived.

### Failure containment matrix [all claims; D01–D15 as applicable in §3]

| Failure                                                         | Required containment                                                                  | Recovery evidence                                                  |

No single stage completion asserts all 21 claims have shipped. Where a dependency
cannot be proven, the dependent feature remains conditional. Failure in a
noncritical optional expansion may return fewer supplementary authorized edges
or occurrences with explicit completeness state. This is **not** permission for
a partial selected-shard ranking: any selected shard's failure/timeout fails the
whole search. Failure in authorization, revocation or critical policy validation
denies the affected path.

### Evidence required at each stage boundary

**V2 entry (V2-\*; linked v1 dependencies in §3):**

- Name the baseline commit, corpus/index generation, model/pipeline and effective
  principal/scope. A synthetic fixture can supply these now; runtime integration
  needs actual records rather than document/issue closure.
- For the affected read paths, settle the logical/physical classification mapping
  and fence legacy monolith/replica routes identified by #8479. Use the restricted
  scope if the full boundary cannot yet be proven.
- Demonstrate version/occurrence resolution, suppression/purge and current-rights
  checks for any evidence returned. Full trace claims additionally wait on #6027;
  missing-vs-buried production claims wait on coverage and the fresh-run seam.
- Record a measurable rollout budget and its owner: candidate/byte/time caps,
  retention, resource use and minimum relevance/latency requirements. Fixtures
  must pass all decidable invariants; operational thresholds are chosen before
  the trial, not invented as a result in this document.

**V2 exit / V3 entry (V3-\*; relevant V2 predecessor in §3):** produce the fixture
and runtime conformance receipts, a versioned judgment/feedback set with consent
and provenance, train/test separation and source-discovery limits, the fixed
comparison configurations and query slices, and a rehearsed rollback. The
responsible experiment owner predeclares the metric, non-inferiority margin,
rare/sensitive cohort regression limits, sample size/uncertainty method and stop
conditions. A statistically inconclusive or unrepresentative set does not permit
adaptation; keep the deterministic path. Graph and curation can progress without
learning when their own V2 predecessor and policy proofs hold.

**V3 exit / V4 entry (V4-\*; relevant V3 predecessor in §3):** show sustained,
audited V3 operation over a declared workload and fault set; current source-rights
and derived-artifact revocation handling; bounded action/delegation policy and
operator kill switch; sufficient randomized support for each adaptive action
being evaluated; and held-out quality/cost/latency results with uncertainty.
Propensity evidence is required for adaptive-ranking causal claims, not an
arbitrary prerequisite for an unrelated portable evidence receipt. Reversible
placement needs a verified recovery target; usefulness deletion remains human.

**V4 exit:** is evidence that the _named_ bounded policy meets its declared
requirements, never a license for general autonomy. Expand an action set, source
class, audience, purpose or budget only through a new versioned evaluation and
policy decision. Each gate receipt names passed, failed, unknown and excluded
claims; no single `v4 enabled` flag can stand in for this inventory.

Feedback correction/retraction, exposure and normalization stay with #6027's
follow-ons. New experiments consume those interfaces instead of silently adding
a feedback writer to a read-only search service. A missing prerequisite is a
named blocked edge, not a reason to guess its contract or wait on an unnamed
human oracle. The chosen fallback is the narrower working capability.
