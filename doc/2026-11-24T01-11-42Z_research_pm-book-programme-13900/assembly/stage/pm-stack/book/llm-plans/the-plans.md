# The plans we use

This chapter lists every plan and pool in the registry: 30 rows, counted in the evidence pack assembled 2026-11-23. Every fee, window, quota and reset in this chapter carries the date its source was read, in the same sentence or the same table row. No figure in this chapter is current. A figure with an old read date is labelled stale with its age. A `limit: null` entry in the plan-facts block means no established fact exists for that limit (registry rule, read 2026-11-23).

The assistant Muse's own Power subscription is not a session pool, and no session draws on it (POOL MAP, registry, read 2026-11-23).

How the plans connect to routes and tiers is the subject of [Pools, routes and the switchboard](pools-and-routes.md) and [Models by tier, and why](models-by-tier.md). The state of each pool at its last dated read is in [Walls, resets and quota doctrine](walls-and-resets.md).
