## The doctrine

- Burn to 100% at the reset. "Use every pool all the way to 100%" (Drew, 2026-10-27, registry, read 2026-11-23). Pools reach 100% at their reset; at a known 100% the pool is closed until its reset or a fresh read shows it below 100% (registry, read 2026-11-23). Some providers reset early; OpenAI does at times (registry, read 2026-11-23).
- Never funds. Plan quotas only (POOL MAP, read 2026-11-23): "A 402/quota error is a wall, never a fallback to balances or PAYG. At most ONE retry on the same pool… When every plan is walled, the session STOPS." (POOL MAP, read 2026-11-23). The three TEMPORARY pay-as-you-go tails in [The plans we use](the-plans.md) (rows 25–27, read 2026-11-23) are Drew's dated exception, ordered 2026-11-23 13:20 CST and TEMPORARY until he lifts the order (read 2026-11-23); the exception is not a repeal of the rule (pack §2, read 2026-11-23).
- Measurement gate. No placement, switch or dispatch without a quota read at most 60 minutes old for that pool (registry and efficiency plan, read 2026-11-23). Burns over 1 hour re-check hourly and at every failover (registry and efficiency plan, read 2026-11-23). A book figure carries its read date in the same sentence for the same reason: a quota read older than 60 minutes does not gate a dispatch, and a dated book figure is a record of a read, not a state claim (pack §5, read 2026-11-23).
- Unmeasured and stale pools are stated as unmeasured and stale: two pools were unmeasured or stale at their freshest read dated 2026-11-23 (Claude, rate-limited gatherer, last measured 2026-11-23T03:00Z; Meta, no clean read for the new weekly window, freshest measurement 2026-11-21T04:45Z), and every statement in this chapter about their state on 2026-11-23 says so in its row (pack §5, read 2026-11-23).
- Burn by expiry, OpenAI last. "Burn the soonest-expiring pool first; OpenAI is the final reserve. Heavier models are justified when a reset nears and quota would otherwise expire — expiring quota only, never money." (Registry, read 2026-11-23.) Effort guide rule 6, read 2026-11-23, states the same order: "OpenAI is the final reserve. Never drain it until every other pool is used up."
- Walls scope to the narrowest true scope. Pool-level verdicts were abolished on 2026-11-07 (rule dated 2026-11-07 23:02 CST, read 2026-11-23); a wall benches a model or a provider, not a pool. The full record is in [Pools, routes and the switchboard](pools-and-routes.md) and in [Benchmarks and real-use evidence](benchmarks-and-evidence.md) entry B7.
- Free-first ordering interacts with walls in one recorded way in the pack dated 2026-11-23: the free/paid alias split (entry B3, dated 2026-11-23) keeps a paid-side wall from benching free models on the same credential source (read 2026-11-23).
