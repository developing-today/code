## 2. Models in use by role/tier — and why

### 2.1 The governing rule (read this before the table)

Roles do **not** name models. Stewardship Decision 008 (Drew, 2026-10-25), quoted at
the head of EFF:

> "roles state requirements only (strength tier = hard minimum-intelligence floor,
> plus a speed flag); the model is picked live at dispatch time; named-model pins in
> agent/role defaults are banned. This plan's ladder and quota rules remain the
> resolver's rulebook for that live pick."

### 2.3 Cross-tier rules that decide the actual pick (all quoted from the sources)

- **Cheapest sufficient first, measured fresh.** EFF's one rule: "Never use more model
  than the task needs, and never spend money to keep working." The measurement gate
  (REG + EFF): no placement, switch, or dispatch without a quota read **≤60 minutes
  old** for that pool; burns over 1h re-check hourly and at every failover.
- **Burn to 100%, then closed.** "Use every pool all the way to 100%" (Drew,
  2026-10-27, REG); at a known 100% the pool is CLOSED until its reset or a fresh
  read shows it below 100% ("some providers reset early — OpenAI does sometimes").
- **NEVER FUNDS.** "Plan quotas only. A 402/quota error is a wall, never a fallback
  to balances or PAYG. At most ONE retry on the same pool… When every plan is
  walled, the session STOPS." (REG POOL MAP.) The three TEMPORARY PAYG tails in
  §1 rows 25–27 are Drew's dated exception (2026-11-23 13:20 CST), not a repeal.
- **Burn-by-expiry; OpenAI is the final reserve.** "Burn the soonest-expiring pool
  first; OpenAI is the final reserve. Heavier models are justified when a reset
  nears and quota would otherwise expire — expiring quota only, never money."
  (REG.) GUIDE rule 6 states the same: "OpenAI is the final reserve. Never drain
  it until every other pool is used up."
- **Free-first.** "Free models sit in front wherever they can serve — free legs lead
  every pool their capability fits; plan/paid legs follow." (Drew, 2026-11-23,
  standing rule; executed as the free-first ordering pass in PR #13691, with the
  recorded exceptions in §2.2.)
- **Gates.** Astra (GPT-6 via the ChatGPT pool): "GATED — only when Aster calls for
  it, or with a specific reason logged at time of use" (REG/EFF). Antigravity
  Argon: "use sparingly — the expensive Gemini tier (price doubles 2026-12-01).
  Prefer Flash tiers" (REG ladder). Cloudflare gateway: "ONE session at a time,
  nothing important" (REG) — and in fact disabled fleet-wide (see §1 row 28).
- **Effort is part of the model choice.** GUIDE rules 1–3: effort is always set
  explicitly ("A turn running variant 'default' is a process failure"); the unit
  of decision is the (model, effort) pair; a hot prompt cache heavily weights
  staying on the current pair. GUIDE §5 sizing: flagships medium; mid tier
  medium–high; small/cheap high–max.
- **Family preference, all else equal** (REG, Drew 2026-10-24): "claude first, then
  openai; gemini sits wherever the specific job fits it" — but "right-sizing beats
  family preference", and where the ladder and the older family text disagree,
  "this ladder wins".
