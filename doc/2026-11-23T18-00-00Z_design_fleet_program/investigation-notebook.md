## Proposed Fleet charter

**Purpose.** Fleet lets an enrolled seat discover suitable work, acquire authority to act, run work on a compatible surface, exchange messages, and leave a verifiable result that another seat can resume. The multicall binary composes the shared interfaces. The brokers own the facts that require coordination across seats. A seat's kind describes its capabilities and environment. A recorded grant determines what it may do. [Founding design and operating rulings in the commission](https://github.com/developing-today/code/issues/13272)

**Principles.** Any agent may present itself to claim a job. A new lane auto-registers, and task-specific requirements and active ownership govern eligibility. A plan quota authorizes only included use. Meter failure leaves the pool eligible through an already verified plan-only route. No automated decision may expose funds. A software change is finished when it is verified and merged. Operational acceptance adds the named deployed generation, consumer and observation. A service should expose enough provenance for its operator to distinguish these states.

| Responsibility | Authority | Evidence delivered to a consumer |
|---|---|---|
| Queue, leases, dependencies, disposition | Queue broker and its transactional store | Task identity, holder and lease, disposition, event and observation watermark |
| Placement and remote runs | Seat selects a surface; provider owns execution state; broker mirrors sessions | Decision inputs and policy revision, provider run/attempt, terminal receipt and artifacts |
| Seat lifecycle and messages | Enrolment profile and bridge today; proposed broker-bound registry for identity and grants | Verified versus reported identity, capabilities, last observation, delivery and acknowledgement |
| Secrets and laptop operations | Secret provider, scoped broker and local continuity policy; service registry owns activation | Credential metadata and freshness, authorized probe result, registry generation and executable digest |
| Plan quotas | pm-stack gatherer and its admission contract | Meter timestamp, reset window, quality, reason and policy revision |
| Search, records and release | Atlas indexes; Fleet owns client behavior. Source, release, bootstrap, deployment and Pages resolve independently. | Index generation and ACL scope, immutable SHA and digest per release stage, source for every status |

**Roadmap.** First resolve the measured repair and publication gaps and correct published operator guidance. Next introduce durable receipts, fencing, per-seat grants, quota provenance and the status read model. Verify each against existing PM, Atlas and ID owners. Use measured concurrency, recovery and cache behavior to change defaults. Keep implementation, as-built documentation and acceptance evidence in the same change stream. Link the eventual archived charter from the Fleet README. #2055 remains the expansion program and #9196 owns the PM orchestration design DAG. ### Lease duration and early renewal



The archived source documents the four-hour `agent next` grant as a deliberate response to observed one-to-three-hour tasks in an intake flow with no automatic heartbeat. A normal grant defaults to 45 minutes. A default heartbeat requests 15 minutes in both client and broker, and the broker computes `now + 15 minutes`. At five minutes into a four-hour lease this yields an expiry at minute 20, moving it earlier by 220 minutes. On a 45-minute grant it moves it earlier by 25 minutes. These are calculations from source, separate from any production observation. Proposed invariant for a valid grant is `new_deadline = max(current_deadline, writer_now + allowed_extension)` within a bounded renewal policy. `park`, `handoff`, and `release` can relinquish the claim explicitly. Measure abandoned-grant recovery and background heartbeat overhead by seat capability before changing either initial grant default. [Intake rationale](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/4cfceaa/pkgs/fleet/crates/fleet-agent/src/next.rs#L63-L72) · [Constants](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/4cfceaa/pkgs/fleet/crates/fleet-queue/src/turso_queue/claim.rs#L487-L501) · [Renewal](https://github.com/developing-today/code/blob/4cfceaa/pkgs/fleet/crates/fleet-queue/src/turso_queue/lifecycle.rs#L1568-L1602)

The archived broker's `placement-put` upsert unconditionally replaces `record`, `state`, `surface`, `submitted_by`, `run_id` and `updated_at` on a handle collision. A late older update can erase a terminal observation from the broker itself. A third seat with no cached copy then sees only the overwritten record.
