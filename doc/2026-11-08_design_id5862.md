# Design decision record: board subscription, discovery & feed integration

Issue: #5862 (follow-on design from parent #5680, decomposition item 6).
Programme: epic #2056. Boards v1: #2054. Status: decided (this record),
2026-11-08. Implementation is a separate, later decision — this record
settles the shape only, per the parent issue's filing note.

## Scope and non-goals

This record settles how shared board posts surface to people other than
the poster: (1) subscription — how a viewer follows a board; (2)
discovery — how a viewer finds boards and posts they do not already
follow; (3) feed integration — how posts from followed boards appear in
a viewer's feed.

Not settled here, and not re-decided: post semantics (reference vs
snapshot) and posting permissions (#5856); curated-board submission
queues (#5857); theme format and precedence (#5858); the live-object
embed contract (#5859); board moderation hooks and roles (#5861, on
#835). Where this record depends on one of those, the dependency is
named and the other ticket's eventual decision plugs in unchanged.

## Decision 1 — Subscription is an explicit, private follow object

- Following a board creates a first-class subscription object:
  `{subscriber, board, created_at, notify_mode, muted}`. It is created
  only by an explicit follow action by the subscriber. Nothing else
  creates one: not posting to a board, not having a post reposted, not
  being a board member, not viewing a board.
- A subscription is the subscriber's private data. Board owners see an
  aggregate follower count only where the board is discoverable
  (Decision 2); they never see the follower list of a non-public board,
  and no surface exposes who follows what to third parties.
- Unfollow deletes the subscription and takes effect immediately on
  every surface (feed, discovery "following" shelf, notifications).
  Mute is a subscription state, not a separate system: a muted
  subscription keeps the follow (and the board in the viewer's board
  list) but removes its posts from the feed and silences its
  notifications.
- Subscribing never grants anything. A subscription to a board the
  subscriber cannot read is allowed to exist (e.g. a follow placed
  while access was held) but surfaces nothing until read authority
  exists, and confers no read authority itself.

## Decision 2 — Discovery is authority-filtered, in three shelves

Discovery shows a viewer exactly three shelves, in this order:

1. **Following** — boards the viewer subscribes to, most recent post
   first.
2. **Shared with you** — boards the viewer's read authority reaches
   that they do not follow, most recent share/post first.
3. **Public directory** — boards whose owner has set
   `discoverability = public`. Public is an explicit per-board opt-in
   by the owner; the default for every board is not-public, and
   changing it is an owner action recorded on the board.

- Search and directory listing index only what the requesting viewer
  may read: board title/description and post metadata for public
  boards; nothing at all for non-public boards the viewer cannot read.
  There is no global index of private board metadata.
- Ranking inside a shelf is recency only (latest post/share time).
  There is no engagement-based ranking, no trending computation, and
  no personalised recommendation in this design; adding any of those
  later is a new design decision, not an implementation detail.
- Discovery never surfaces a post the viewer cannot read, even as a
  teaser: no title, no excerpt, no thumbnail of an unreadable post
  appears in any shelf.

## Decision 3 — The feed is a viewer-side, chronological aggregation of references

- The feed is computed for a viewer from: their unmuted subscriptions,
  plus boards they own. It is a view, not a stored stream: no per-user
  feed copies are materialised, so unfollow, mute, revocation, and
  deletion take effect on the next render with no cleanup pass.
- Ordering is strictly chronological by post time (newest first).
  Reposts surface at repost time with the full attribution chain
  (original author, original board, reposting board) per the parent
  issue's repost model; the same post arriving via several followed
  boards appears once, with all arrival paths listed.
- A feed item is a reference to the post, rendered under the post
  semantics #5856 settles (live reference or snapshot). This record
  deliberately does not pick that default; the feed stores no copy of
  its own, so either #5856 outcome plugs in without a feed redesign.
- Read authority is checked at render time, every render. If the
  underlying object or post is withdrawn, deleted, or the viewer's
  authority lapses, the item disappears from the feed — including from
  positions the viewer has already scrolled past on a later visit.
  There is no "you saw it once, so it stays" rule.
- Playable posts render in the feed as their static fallback
  (per the #5859 embed contract) with a clear "open on board" path;
  live embeds run only on the board surface itself, never inline in a
  feed. Themes render per #5858 precedence; a viewer's accessibility
  overrides beat every theme, in the feed as everywhere.
- Feed is not notification. Following a board puts its posts in the
  feed and nothing more. Notifications are a separate opt-in per
  subscription (`notify_mode`: off (default), digest, immediate),
  set at follow time and changeable after; the default for every new
  subscription is off. Post/discussion surfaces are the discussion
  design's, per the parent — the feed adds no comment system.

## Invariants implementation must hold

1. **No authority widening.** Subscription, discovery, and feed never
   widen any object's or board's read audience beyond its own
   read-authority (the parent issue's posting rule, applied to every
   surfacing path). Any implementation that needs a wider audience
   must go through the owner's explicit widening confirmation, which
   lives in #5856's flow, not here.
2. **Render-time authority check.** Every surface re-checks read
   authority when rendering; cached or indexed data is never trusted
   as proof of authority.
3. **Revocation is total and prompt.** Unfollow, mute, object
   withdrawal, post removal (via #5861/#835 moderation), and authority
   loss all remove the item from feed and discovery on the next
   render, with no residual metadata (title/excerpt/thumbnail) left
   behind.
4. **Subscription privacy.** Follower identity is visible to no one
   but the follower, except aggregate counts on public boards.
5. **No silent subscriptions.** No code path creates a subscription
   as a side effect of another action.
6. **Chronology honesty.** Feed order is post time; no ranking signal
   may reorder it without a new recorded design decision.

## Alternatives considered and rejected

- **Algorithmic / ranked feed** (engagement or interest ranking):
  rejected — needs behavioural profiling the platform has not agreed
  to collect, breaks chronology honesty, and is disproportionate for
  boards' current scale. Recency shelves deliver the discovery value
  without it.
- **Auto-subscribe on interaction** (posting to, being posted on, or
  viewing a board follows it): rejected — violates subscription
  privacy and predictability; produces feeds users never asked for
  and cannot explain.
- **Materialised per-user feed streams** (fan-out on write): rejected
  — makes revocation and deletion a distributed cleanup problem and
  directly endangers invariant 3; viewer-side aggregation keeps one
  authority check in one place.
- **Feed copies posts (snapshot-by-default for feed only):** rejected
  — would silently fork #5856's post-semantics decision and keep
  withdrawn content visible. The feed references; #5856 decides what
  a reference renders as.
- **Global public directory of all boards** (opt-out instead of
  opt-in): rejected — board titles and membership are metadata owners
  have not consented to publish; opt-in public is the only default
  consistent with invariant 1.
- **Notifications on by default for follows:** rejected — follow is a
  reading preference, not a request for interruption; default-off
  with digest/immediate opt-ins covers both audiences.

## Parent-decision check

Nothing in this record changes a decision in parent #5680. It adopts
the parent's positions as constraints: posting never widens read
audience (parent §1, restated as invariant 1); repost attribution
chains (parent §1); discussion lives in the discussion design, not in
boards (parent §5); moderation of surfaced posts is #5861/#835's
system, invoked here only as a removal input to invariant 3. The
parent's open question about board discovery/subscription ("following
a board, board posts in a feed, mute/unfollow", parent §5) is the
question this record answers.

## Follow-on

Implementation is filed as a single linked follow-on issue from this
record (subscription object + three-shelf discovery + chronological
