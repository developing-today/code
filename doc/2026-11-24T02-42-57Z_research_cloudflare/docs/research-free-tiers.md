# Cloudflare — Services With a Free Tier: Limits, Type, and When Charges Start

**Research date:** 2026-10-20
**Scope:** Every Cloudflare service with a genuine free offer, across the website plans, Zero Trust, and the developer platform — not just Workers.
**Method:** Official Cloudflare sources first (`developers.cloudflare.com` pricing/limits pages and `cloudflare.com/plans`). Every row carries its source URL. Where only a secondary source could be found, or official pages conflict, the row is flagged **UNVERIFIED** — do not budget from an UNVERIFIED row without re-checking the official page.
**Prices:** USD, as published on the cited page on the research date. Nothing here is invented; a missing price means the official page did not publish one.

## How to read the "Type" column — the trial-vs-free distinction

| Code                                      | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **(a) Perpetual free tier**               | A standalone $0 plan / allowance that renews indefinitely (daily or monthly reset). No payment, no expiry. On most Workers-platform free tiers, exceeding the limit is a **hard stop** (operations fail until reset), not a bill.                           |
| **(b) Free allowance inside a paid plan** | $0 only because you already pay for a plan (usually Workers Paid, $5/mo minimum, or a Pro/Business zone plan). There is **no way to use the free amount without the paid plan**. Overage is metered automatically — this is where surprise bills come from. |
| **(c) Time-limited TRIAL**                | Free only for a fixed window, then it charges or stops. **Almost nothing in Cloudflare's catalogue is actually a (c).** See §4 — the common confusions (Stream on Pro/Business, Email Sending, Containers) are (b), not trials.                             |

> **Headline finding:** Cloudflare essentially does not do time-limited trials on self-serve products. Its "free" is either (a) perpetual with a hard cap, or (b) an included allowance inside a $5+ paid plan with metered overage. The risk model is therefore not "trial expires" — it is "hard stop at midnight UTC" on (a), and "unbounded metered overage" on (b).

On **Workers Paid ($5/mo account minimum)**, the _same products_ switch to large included monthly allowances + metered overage. Moving to Paid is what converts a capped free tier into an unbounded bill.

## 5. What this means for a $5/month (Workers Paid) budget — quick synthesis

- **Safely free forever (type a, hard caps, cannot bill you while on Free plans):** DNS/CDN/DDoS/Universal SSL, WAF free ruleset + Bot Fight Mode, Turnstile, Web Analytics, Zaraz ≤1M events/mo, DMARC Management, Email Routing, Zero Trust ≤50 users + Tunnel, Workers/KV/D1/Durable Objects/Queues/Hyperdrive/Workflows/Analytics Engine/Workers Logs within daily caps, Pages ≤500 builds/mo, R2 within monthly allowance (verify billing behaviour — see note), Workers AI ≤10k Neurons/day, Browser Rendering ≤10 min/day, Images ≤5,000 transformations/mo, AI Gateway core, Realtime ≤1,000 GB egress/mo.
- **Free only because you pay $5 (type b — metered overage risk):** Containers allowance, Email Sending 3,000/mo, Vectorize (if Paid-gated), Artifacts, Workers Logpush, plus the Paid included allowances for Workers/KV/D1/DO/Queues themselves.
- **Free only inside a $20+ zone plan (type b):** Stream's 100 stored / 10,000 delivered minutes (Pro/Business).
- **Never free, despite frequent claims:** Load Balancing ($5+), Argo ($5+), Waiting Room (Business+), Images storage ($5/100k), Stream standalone, Log Explorer (per official docs), Cache Reserve, Snippets, Pipelines/R2 SQL.
- **Not free but not a markup either:** Registrar — registry cost only, $0 Cloudflare markup, same at renewal.
