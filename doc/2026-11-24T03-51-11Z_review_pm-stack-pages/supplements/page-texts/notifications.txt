Notifications

Weekly sent-vs-acted-on roll-up over the alert throat's send ledger (alerts.notification_sends), rolling four weeks ending 2026-11-24 03:34:02Z. Read-only: this page measures; it never changes a notification preference or default. Machine-first: /api/notifications.json.

How acted-on is counted (attribution)

A send counts as acted-on when the incident's Ack lands within 24 hours of the send: Ack is the exactly-once transition that stamps the send's acted_on_at, so it is the durable acted-on signal. Only ledger rows with outcome sent count as sends — suppressed (quiet hours / dedupe), gone, and error attempts never reached a device and never enter the denominator, and no send is inferred from delivered/escalated incident events. What this cannot attribute: reading a notification without acking, acting on the incident through another surface (GitHub, chat, a later dashboard visit that does not ack), and sends to subscriptions that were later removed — those count as sent-but-not-acted even when a human did see them. Test sends (class test) are real ledger rows and are reported as their own class.
