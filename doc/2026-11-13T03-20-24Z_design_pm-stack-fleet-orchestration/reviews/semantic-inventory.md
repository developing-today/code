However, the source ledger’s zero-unmatched-semantic-span condition and its 3571 occurrence count prove only mechanical coverage. This review found an actionable locator defect and a few places where the curated record/reporting contract could still conceal lost semantics. In particular, the separate command inventory is required evidence, not a substitute for H-R024/H-R031’s full compatibility and acceptance obligations. Do not use zero unresolved spans as a semantic-completeness claim.
