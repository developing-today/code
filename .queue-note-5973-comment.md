**What the note covers** (issue #6004's done-looks-like):

1. **The arc, dated:** wizards hold everything → 1992 *LambdaMOO Takes a New Direction* (social decisions devolved, technical power kept) → 1993 petition/ballot + archons → the Mr. Bungle capability-abuse incident → 1996 *Another Direction* (wizards retake social authority).
2. **Reserved-authority table (explicit, six rows):** installing/replacing the world program, capability ceilings and grants, the admin token itself, the resource envelope, revoking a participant's capability tree, host-level serving/delisting and stopping the world — each with *why it cannot be delegated* and *what already exists in `pkgs/id`* (`WorldScopes::BUILDER`/`MODERATOR`, `WorldLimits.server_caps`, `authorize_admin`, `JournalEntry::*`).
3. **Delegable set:** content rules and thresholds, who moderates, appeals of content decisions, approval of peer submissions, adoption/parameterization of moderation packs — with the one hard condition that a delegated decision must act at the speed of the harm or be backed by a reserved path that can.
4. **Quota as governance:** the wizard quota-review practice, mapped onto `WorldLimits` / `WorldExecutionProfile::resolve`.
5. **Mr. Bungle mapped to id:** per-user function mapping (#5967) attribution is reserved; `WorldScopes::DELEGATE` attenuation is always a strict subset; presentation may change how a participant *looks*, never who they *are* or what they *did*.

Two invariants fall out: no governance body may widen its own authority, and every exercise of reserved authority is logged against what it changed (reusing the boundary-audit decision log).
